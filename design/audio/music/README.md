# Music

## Summary

About 30 tracks: menu and hangar themes, two level themes per act, boss themes, jingles, and
the ending. Tracks loop seamlessly and switch with short crossfades. A simple two-layer
intensity system makes busy sections hit harder without needing adaptive composition
everywhere.

### Motifs

- **Main motif** (humanity / Lancer): a rising 5-note brass phrase on scale degrees
  **1 – 5 – 8 – 9 – ♭10** (♮10 in major keys), rhythm long-short-long-short-long. In D minor:
  D4 · A4 · D5 · E5 · F5. It grew out of the horn call in "Coalition Rising". It appears in the
  title (brass statement, then in F major in the B section), the hangar (a relaxed 5-8-9-♭10
  fragment in the flute), act 1 and the ending.
- **Choir motif** (Vrell): a wordless descending vocal line on degrees
  **8 – ♭7 – ♭6 – 5**, a tritone drop to **♭2**, resolving to **1**. In E: E5 · D5 · C5 · B4 · F4 · E4.
  The tritone drop and the ♭2→1 half step make it alien. It is sung by a formant "choir" and
  doubled by low brass; augmented (half speed) in breakdowns. First used in the Vrell boss
  theme.
- **Vorne's motif** (Ascendancy): a cold 4-note arpeggio on degrees **1 – ♭3 – 5 – 7**, the
  minor-major-seventh chord whose unresolved major 7th sounds "wrong" over a minor key.
  Stately rhythm: dotted quarter – eighth – half | whole. In B minor: B3 · D4 · F♯4 · A♯4.
  It is played by cold low brass (often with an organ an octave down) and, at speed, by
  machines: the arpeggio sequencers of the belt theme and the 16th riff of the Ascendancy boss
  theme. In the final boss it is sung by the Choir: Vorne, absorbed. It follows the twist timing in
  [story](../../story/README.md): a faint background layer from the first hints in Act 3 (the
  Act 3 B theme and the L19 Revenant Walker fight) and in Act 4 (L27–28), then in the open from
  Act 5, when Vorne's broadcast reveals the Ascendancy.
  In track 9 "Grey Signal" (Act 3 B, D minor) it is not brass but a thin whistle heard through a
  radio band-pass, D4 · F4 · A4 · C♯5: an intercepted human signal (round 36, S.1 = b).

### Loops and transitions

- Every loop has an intro section (played once) and a loop section with sample-accurate loop
  points. Delivery format (as in the round-02 concept files): intro + loop in one file, loop
  points as Vorbis comments `LOOPSTART` / `LOOPLENGTH` in samples, followed by a short
  fade-out tail that only players ignoring the loop points reach.
- Level → boss: boss warning stinger replaces the level track (crossfade 0.5 s), then the boss
  track starts on the downbeat.
- Level → debrief: 1 s fade, then the mission complete jingle; track 29 "Stand Down" comes in on the
  jingle's downbeat, 5.714 s after it starts (three bars at 126 BPM, while its last chord rings out),
  and loops under the debrief and its act summary page. A debrief without the jingle (Level 13's
  played inside the level) starts track 29 at once. Leaving the debrief fades it out over 0.5 s before
  the next screen and its music (the hangar's, the mission select's, or the act outro's fanfare).
- Death: level music cuts instantly (with a short low-pass sweep), then the mission failed sting.

### Intensity layers

Level themes are delivered as two synced stems: **base** and **intensity** (extra drums and
lead). The intensity stem fades in (1 s) when a scripted level section asks for it, and fades out
4 s after calm returns. **As built** the switch is by section only (a level's `start_section`,
`full_section` and `stems`); driving it by the on-screen density is **not built** (no level asks
for it). Planned (M5 part C): a **run-time hook** from the sortie's state, the music data's
`full_on`: `hold` (the full mix while a hold zone runs, 1 s in as it starts, 4 s out after it ends)
and `collapse` (the full mix from Level 09's collapse to the end).

**Scripted duck** (planned, M5 part D, Level 10's scripted loss): on a level event the theme ducks
by a set amount for a set time (Level 10: **−6 dB for 3 s** from the lance at t=118, no sting).
It does not stack with the radio's duck (−4 dB while a voice plays): the lower of the two applies.
The music data's `duck` (`on`, the event; `db`; `seconds`).

**Level by section** (planned, M5 part F, Level 12's eye; user decision F5 = a): a level's music
can set the theme's level per section (`section_db`: from that section until the next entry, a
1 s ramp), as `start_db` does for the start. Level 12: the ambience alone in section 1, the base
stem from section 2, the full mix in the storm wall (section 4), the **base stem at −6 dB in the
eye** (section 5, under the Choir's stage sound) and the full mix at full level again for the
breakout (section 6); the switches themselves are the existing `stems` by section. The radio's duck
applies on top, as on a `start_db` start. No new stem or render (a third "eye" stem was rejected).

The concept tracks are full mixes; round 11 delivered the first stem pair. A level that asks for
the base stem until a section (Level 01 until section 3) plays the base stem from its music start
and crossfades in 1 s to the full mix at that section: both streams are decoded in lockstep and
mixed sample by sample, so they stay aligned across the loop points and the fade never clicks. A level
can start the theme quieter (`start_db`): Level 01 plays the base stem from the launch at −6 dB
under the ambience and the launch rail, rising to full in 2 s at the section 2 transition.

Round 11 proposes the first pair, for "Coalition Rising": a base stem rendered from the same
pass as the chosen full mix (same length, loop points and gain), so the game can crossfade from
the base stem to the full mix sample-aligned; the full mix is the intensity layer's "on" state.

  [homefront-full-r08-a](concept/homefront-full-r08-a.ogg) rendered by `tools/art/themes.py` (key

#### Track 8 "Red Dust Run" in production (M7 part B)

#### Track 9, the Act 3 B theme: brief

Where track 8 runs, track 9 stalks: the same desert, slower and colder, with the first trace of
something that is not Vrell under it. It follows the round-36 sketch **b "Grey Signal"** (user
decision S.1 = b, 2026-10-13; [concept/act3-b-theme-r36-b.ogg](concept/act3-b-theme-r36-b.ogg), its
brief in [concept/prompts.md](concept/prompts.md));
the title is "Grey Signal". The first brief's 122 BPM in B phrygian is dropped (Q.24e).

- **Tempo and key**: **125 BPM, D minor**, 10 % swing; the chords `Dm9 Dm9 B♭maj7 B♭maj7 Gm9 Gm9
  A7(♭9) A7(♭9)` (the breakdown `Dm9 Dm9 B♭maj7 A7(♭9)`). Vorne's motif sits in D minor here (D · F ·
  A · C♯, see *Motifs*): its C♯ grinds against the C of the D minor-ninth, so the motif sounds wrong
  without being loud.
- **Sound**: **dub techno**: syncopated minor-ninth and major-seventh chord stabs through a
  dotted-eighth ping-pong delay and a long reverb, their filter opening through the loop; a deep sub
  bass; rimshots and swung 16th hats (open hats in D); **track 8's hand drums and shaker** from the
  B section on, so the act's two themes are one family; a dark pad; the dust wind swelling in the
  breakdown. **No bubbly or popping sounds**: no resonant "bloop" plucks, no water-drop or popping
  percussion.
- **Form** (production ≈ 3:00; the sketch 65.8 s): an intro played once (wind, the filtered groove
  opening up) → A, the groove → B, the stabs and hand drums → breakdown (stabs, pad and sub alone, a
  kick fill back in) → D, the full groove, looping back to A; the production extends the sketch's
  28-bar loop to about 3:00.
- **Stems**: three, sample-aligned from one render as round 11's: **base** (kick, sub, pad, shaker
  and hand drums, the wind), **full** (the base plus the hats' top, rimshots and the stabs), and
  **motif**, Vorne's motif layer below. Delivery as every theme (OGG Vorbis q6, −14 LUFS full mix,
  true peak ≤ −1 dBTP, loop comments, `SOURCE`).

#### Vorne's motif layer (Levels 18–19)

The motif is planted where the grey, human-built machines are, and nowhere else in the act: from the
Ghost Drones' first sight in Level 18 (t=69.5, **hint 1**) and under the Revenant Walker in Level 19,
alone over its wreck (**hint 2**). It is **muted in Level 21** (the Colossus is pure Vrell) and
returns in Act 4 (L27–L28).

- **The layer** (as in the chosen sketch b): an **intercepted radio signal**, a thin whistle voice
  through a radio band-pass (700 Hz – 2.6 kHz) over a swell of band-noise static (no crackle), the
  motif on D4 · F4 · A4 · C♯5, one statement every 4 bars from bar 8 (≈ 7.7 s apart in the sketch;
  the production may space them wider); mixed about **18–20 dB under the full mix** (17 in the
  breakdown): a player notices it the second time, not the first. A human transmission where none
  should be.
- **Playback** (a, decided in round 36, Q.24d): the motif stem is a **third stream** decoded in lockstep with
  the base and full stems and added on top of their crossfade, with its own level: in over 2 s, out
  over 4 s. A level's music data gets a `motif` list (by section, time or event: `on`, `off` or
  `alone`); `alone` fades the base and full stems out over 2 s and leaves the motif over the
  ambience. This is not Level 12's rejected third "eye" stem (F5): that one could be had by a
  level change of the existing stems (`section_db`), while the motif is musical content that must be
  absent everywhere else and heard alone once. Alternative b: four premixed files of one render
  (base, base + motif, full, full + motif) and a fifth, the motif alone, with the player crossfading
  between any two; no third stream, but five files and a more general crossfade. Rejected (b).
