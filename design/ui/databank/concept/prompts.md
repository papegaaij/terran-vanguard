# Databank – concept prompts

Mockups generated by [tools/concept/databank_r82.py](../../../../tools/concept/databank_r82.py)
(`python3 tools/concept/databank_r82.py`, a few seconds). Nothing is painted by hand: the screen is drawn at the game's
960×540 from the real assets (the glass kit's nine-patches and colours, the bitmap fonts, the hangar's tactical map,
the units' production frames, the intel portraits, the gear icons, Dr. Varga's radio portrait) and shown at 1280×720 as
a 1280×720 window shows it (nearest ×2, then a smooth step down). The campaign shown: medium, the hangar before
Level 40. The numbers are the data's (HP and bounty as the databank would compute them). Concept round 82.

Brief for every screen: the out-of-game glass style of the menus and the hangar (translucent navy glass, thin metal
trim, amber selection, cyan values, green for the player's own gear, red for what hurts the player), one page per
entry, no scrolling inside an entry, the list on the left, the key hints at the bottom.

## databank-r82-a

Layout a: the list. Tabs ENEMIES / BOSSES / WEAPONS / SHIP / CONCEPTS with their counts; the list grouped by act
(scrolled to Acts V–VI), names with the first-met level, `ALLY` and `NEW` tags, two `???` rows for Act VI's unknown
units. The entry: the Mirror Interceptor at sensor L3 with its sprite (body, mirror plate and gun pods, frame 0) at 1:1,
the fields, the generated weapon advice with the player's matching gear on the right, Varga's precise tip.

## databank-r82-b

Layout b: the same entry with the list replaced by a grid of the 30×30 intel portraits by act (the cyan callout cells,
the amber one selected, `?` cells for the unknown), the selected unit's name and faction under the grid. The Cracking
Tower and Pump Station have no intel portrait yet: their cells show their sprite scaled down.

## databank-enemy-none-r82-a

The Mirror Interceptor with no sensor suite: the silhouette (the sprite's shape in dark navy, a lit rim, scan lines),
the name and first-met level, every other field `- - - -` with a tag naming the sensor level that shows it, the amber
callout explaining what a suite fills in with a `SENSOR SUITE >` chip, the shop line, Varga's vague line. The top bar's
badge reads `NONE` in red with `SEE SHIP TAB`.

## databank-boss-r82-a

The Iron Sovereign at sensor L3 on the BOSSES tab (bosses, mid-bosses and the Leviathan set piece by act, two `???`
rows for Act VI). The picture is a stand-in for the databank plate: the production base, rim and core frame composed
and scaled to 128 px. Fields, the phase notes (rings, locks, the core's playbook), the advice (anti-ground ×2 on a
ground station, homing and piercing for the batteries, shield-breaker and EMP for the open core), Varga's tip.

## databank-weapon-r82-a

The Tesla Coil Pod on the WEAPONS tab (front, wings, rear, specials with the player's levels, `FITTED` and `NEW` tags):
the large icon at 4×, traits, the player's level and mount, the layers it reaches and the ones it cannot, the L1–L5
table (DPS, draw, cost, reach) with L2 lit, a stand-in test-fire box (the build uses the hangar's own), the met units it
is strong against, a short note.

## databank-sensor-r82-a

The sensor suite's own entry on the SHIP tab (core parts, utilities, escort), the campaign owning none: its costs, draw,
in-flight arrows and easy's bonus, and the ladder side by side: what each level adds to the hangar intel and to the
databank. Varga asks for one.

## databank-access-r82-a

The ways in, 960×600 at 1:1: the hangar's top bar with a fourth command chip `DATA` (the chips narrowed to 54 px at
x 424, the longest mission line beside them), and the main menu with `DATABANK` between MISSIONS and SCORES (the panel
moved up to y 172 for the ninth item).

## databank-plates

Final art, not a mockup: the databank's still plates, 192×192 with a transparent background, written to
`assets/sprites/databank/<slug>.png` (the region `databank/<slug>`) by
[tools/art/databank_plates.py](../../../../tools/art/databank_plates.py) (`python3 tools/art/databank_plates.py`, a few
seconds; `--check` compares every plate with a fresh compose). Brief: each boss (the texts' `bosses`), each unit whose
sprite is over 128 px (the Leviathan, the Abyss Ray) and the Ascendancy concept's cruiser, as the game assembles it from
its production frames, at rest and nose down as it comes at the player, top-lit as the sprites are, in its faction's
livery; nothing redrawn. 1:1 when it fits 184 px, else reduced the way the render path reduces a render; 1-bit alpha,
glows only on the body (no halo, no dither), one 48-colour palette a plate. The sensor-none silhouette is not a file:
the game derives it from the plate's alpha. The review sheet `databank-plates-final-r82-a.png` shows the plates at 1:1
and the silhouettes as a preview.

## databank-capture-r82

Captures from the game itself (step DB3, 2026-11-01), not mockups: the F12 screenshots (960×540, the game's own
resolution) of the build at the worktree's `m10-act-6`, on a real save from `./gradlew :content:playthroughSave
-Plevel=40` (medium, the Level 40 hangar: Acts 1–6 through Level 39 met, kills from 0, the boss "beaten" fix in), the
game run as `--bench 600 --start title --settings <temp>` on its own Xvfb and driven by XTest key presses. The sensor
levels are the same save with a stored `Sensor suite` of level 1, 2 or 3 added to its inventory (a stored suite
counts, round 82 Q.4). Nothing is generated or edited after the capture.

- `databank-capture-r82-none`: the main menu's `DATABANK`, the ENEMIES tab, the Mirror Interceptor at sensor none
  (silhouette, locked fields with their sensor level, the suite callout, Varga's vague line).
- `databank-capture-r82-l1`, `-l2`, `-l3`: the same entry with a suite of level 1 (class, layer, entry edges, kills),
  2 (the picture, HP, mirror heat, bounty, attacks) and 3 (weak points, the weapon advice with the fit's gear, Varga's
  tip).
- `databank-capture-r82-boss`: the BOSSES tab at L3, the Iron Sovereign `BEATEN L35` with its kills at 0, its phases.
- `databank-capture-r82-weapon`: the WEAPONS tab, the Tesla Coil Pod fitted at L2 (levels table, test fire, strong
  against, note).
- `databank-capture-r82-hangar`: the Level 40 hangar with no suite: the `DATA` chip, `TAB DATA` in the hints, the
  intel's `DATABANK: SHIP > SENSOR SUITE` line under `NO SENSOR`.
- `databank-capture-r82-menu`: the main menu with `DATABANK` selected.
