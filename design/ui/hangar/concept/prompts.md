# Hangar / shop – concept prompts

Mockups generated by [tools/concept/ui_r06.py](../../../../tools/concept/ui_r06.py)
(`python3 tools/concept/ui_r06.py hangar`). Each sheet shows the full 960×540 screen at 1×
plus a 2× detail crop. State: the hangar before mission 15 *Olympus Descent* (act 3) with the
gear unlocked by then; front slot selected, Hammer Mortar highlighted in the shop.

Shared direction: the same game as HUD A – brushed blue-violet metal panels with bevels and
rivets, recessed dark LCD wells, green/cyan/amber pixel text in a 5×7 bitmap font, palette B.
Varga's portrait is the style-B comm-screen portrait with retained colour.

Common negative prompt: `modern flat UI, mobile game shop, loot boxes, rounded cards,
glassmorphism, thin modern fonts, photographic background, watermark, misspelled text,
cluttered or illegible layout`

## hangar-r06-a

Three columns (the layout of the hangar README). Prompt: `late 1990s PC shoot'em up hangar
and shop screen, 16:9, brushed blue metal three-column layout with a tab bar (INTEL, SHOP,
LOADOUT, REPAIR, SAVE), a credits readout "CR 12 450" and an orange hazard-striped LAUNCH
button; left column a blueprint grid diagram of a top-down white and blue jet interceptor
sprite with slot boxes for front gun, left and right wing pods, rear gun and an escort ship,
the front slot lit amber, below it a list of ship systems (generator, shield, armour, engine,
special, utilities); centre a shop list of front guns in recessed LCD rows with prices, small
gold diamond trait-match markers, a magenta NEW tag and a greyed locked item, the selected
item "HAMMER MORTAR" with trait chips, DPS, power draw, red and green comparison deltas, BUY
and TEST FIRE buttons and a tiny test-fire preview window; right column an intel panel with a
green-tinted pixel comm-screen portrait of a red-haired female scientist with glasses, quote
text, mission name "OLYMPUS DESCENT", threat profile fields, a density bar, a direction dial
showing attacks from the front, recommended traits with gold diamonds; a segmented amber power
bar along the bottom`

## Round 07 — layout B in the glass menu style

Mockups generated by [tools/concept/ui_r07.py](../../../../tools/concept/ui_r07.py)
(`python3 tools/concept/ui_r07.py [a] [b]`, ~16 s), reusing the glass-panel code of
`ui_r06.py`. User decision (round 06): layout B, but in the style of main menu A — the
bevelled metal is reserved for the in-game HUD. Same state as round 06, now called L15
consistently.

Shared direction: translucent dark-navy glass panels with a thin steel trim and small corner
caps over a pre-rendered scene; white and cyan pixel text in a 5×7 bitmap font, amber caps for
section headers, the amber gradient selection bar with a triangle cursor, amber-outlined chips
for selected choices and steel-outlined chips otherwise; palette B. No brushed metal, rivets,
recessed LCD wells or hazard stripes.

Common negative prompt: `brushed metal panels, rivets, hazard stripes, LCD wells, modern flat
UI, mobile game shop, loot boxes, rounded cards, thin modern fonts, photographic background,
watermark, misspelled text, cluttered or illegible layout`
