Concept round 93: record of the decisions

M11 part F was built (branch m11-act-7, uncommitted; the full ./gradlew check green on 2026-11-07, Acts 1–6's golden flights and replays unchanged): Level 48 "Vorne's End" with the citadel, its gold decks and reactor cores, the rear waves, and the mid-boss Vorne's Chimera rising out of the spire well: three phases, the rear sweep, the capsule opening, and Vorne dying mid-sentence, in his fused voice, over track 34. This round asks, in this order: the fight's sounds in place, track 34 in place, Vorne's voice, part F's art to confirm, the level and the whole fight in play (one real-time recording with sound), and the important calls, each with its recommendation. The recommendation is pre-selected on every row; leave a row alone to keep it.

Decided as trivia, not asked: the Chimera sways on its station (a smooth side-to-side), its playbook keeps it unpredictable; the rear sweep comes at least every second attack in phase 2 (the document said every third) so it shows two to four times; the drop and each tell fire a covering spore pair, so no stretch of the fight is empty; the Dart stream stops when its bay dies; the fight's measure counts a sac "healed back" only after it lost more than a fifth of its health; the Chimera pays 885 credits (recomputed by its formula at the level's money scale, 0.17); the reactor cores' bonus is paid unscaled, as in Levels 43–47; the scroll brakes from 100 to 20 px/s over the last 8 s into the arena instead of stopping dead; the rear wings come up in the half of the field the ship is not in, curving toward the middle; Vorne's archive is a breakable box with the secret's crate; the enemy shots in flight fade out at his death.
