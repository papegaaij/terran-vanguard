Concept round 65: record of the decisions

The review round of M9 part E (roadmap), now built: Level 32 flies end to end on every difficulty and all four routes (the autopilot wins every route; TOWERS DOWN met on medium in every run), the act's terrain level inside Kleopatra: Level 16's walls and islands inside rock with two real forks (the ship's side at an island's tip picks the channel, each channel with its own waves; the unchosen one's guns dormant), the Sentinel Tower behind its hardened collar sweeping its beam (its first sweep the full turn), Strider pairs that topple onto their own guns when a leg goes, the richer channels' blast doors and caches, mining charges, the hall's sealed consortium store (the secret), Level 19's furnace vents in the refinery, TOWERS DOWN, the radio's branch and topple-crush lines (18 voiced lines), track 12 with the tunnel bed and the refinery's foundry roar. The release now ends after Level 32's win ("LEVEL 33 COMING SOON").

The rows ask: part E's production art with game captures (accept or redo; pick a, b or c for how the richer channel reads at a fork, QE-2), eleven sounds (a or b; a plays in the game until you pick), a conveyor stem for track 12 at the forks, and eight calls the build made (the recommendation is pre-selected), the first two important concepts that change the fight: where the towers stand (QE-1) and the full turn first (QE-3). Decided as trivia and only logged (in the parts' Decisions): the overdrive from the last Needler of the t=145.5 column (a ground unit drops only as a group's), a Sentinel's arcs alternating their direction, the intel naming the IN ROCK limit from sensor L1, the Sentinel's bounty 40 and the Strider's 3.1 s kept as approved (BalanceTest's pending list), the Sentinel's death sound added beside the sfx list, the Strider's fall sound played with the leg's snap (its crash at 1.4 s on the crush), the gallery mouths drawn as their own wall images.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.
