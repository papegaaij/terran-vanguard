# Concept round 84 — Act 7 "Beyond the Gate": the design phase

## Summary

**Closed 2026-11-05**: the answers are in the Answer and Outcome columns and in the *Decisions* entry (everything as
recommended and drafted except **Q.10 b**, **E.2 b**, **A.5 b**, **A.6 b**, **A.7 b**, **A.9 b**, **M.1 a, M.3 a and
M.4 changed** (round 85 reworks them), **M.2 b**, the non-default sound picks and **V.2 b**; the nineteen documents
approved).

The design phase of Act 7, the campaign's last act (Levels 43 to 50), is drafted on the branch `m11-act-7` in a separate git worktree; nothing is committed. As for Acts 4 to 6 the drafts follow the **recommended answers**, so every row below has its recommendation pre-selected and you only change what you want changed. The documents are `design: draft`; nothing is built (no `data.yaml` for the act, no Java). v0.4 (Acts 1 to 6) was released on 2026-11-04. The M11 parts' rounds follow from round 85.

The round asks, in your order: the twelve design questions and five extra open items (Q.1 to Q.12, E.1 to E.5), the sixteen judgement calls the drafts made (J.1 to J.16), the artwork (A.1 to A.13: five units, then Vorne's Chimera, the Choir Heart, the echoes, the stills, the closing gate, the ending pages and the portraits, a and b each), the music (M.1 to M.6), the sounds grouped by where they play (S rows, a and b each) and the voices (V.1 and V.2), and last the documents to approve (D.1 to D.19, one row each). The art and sound rows were checked by measure, not by ear: the pre-selected answer on the sound and music rows is a default, not a listened recommendation.

Done before the round, decided and not asked: the Act 7 proposals' credits (176 rows) are in [CREDITS.md](../../../CREDITS.md).

## Review

The review page is built from this file by `build/round84-tools/build_review.py` (untracked); the board is [index.html](index.html) (`python3 tools/concept/board.py 84 --subjects …`). The art rows have sheets (PNG) and, for the units, the bosses, the portraits and the transit, a motion clip each (the board shows the GIFs); the sound rows' worries are measured, not listened to.
