The last round of M8 (roadmap): the fixes round 58 asked for and part I, the close-out. Act 4 is complete: the campaign runs from Level 01 through the Abyssal Maw, the breakout, the act's end and the release's end card, and you can play Act 4 through. A save to start from: ./gradlew :content:playthroughSave -Plevel=22 -Pout=<dir> (the Level 22 hangar, Act 4's intro first) or -Plevel=28 (the Level 28 hangar), then ./gradlew :desktop:run --args="--settings <dir>/settings.properties" and Continue.

Part I (step I3): the playthrough and balance tests run through Level 28 on the levels' own income (part A's temporary income removed), Act 4's variety checked as built, the later tags moved to Act 5, and every voiced Act 4 line checked against a transcript.

The ambience beds

Q.1

The ambience beds' volume

raise or leave.
The ambience beds' volume
raise or leave. You found the beds hard to hear. Measured in the game's mix, every act's beds sit 24.7–29.5 dB under the level's theme: Act 4's Europa beds −27.9 LUFS against the themes' −13.9, the loudest of all acts (Earth arctic as loud; the Martian dust wind the faintest, 29.5 dB under). The game plays every bed at AMBIENCE_VOLUME 0.35 on the effects bus. a: raise every act's beds by 6.0 dB (AMBIENCE_VOLUME 0.35 → 0.70, one constant, every bed alike, so the acts keep their balance; Level 13's and 18's storm swells ride on top as before). b: leave them as a faint background. c: raise them by 9.1 dB (0.35 → 1.0, the most a sound's volume allows; anything louder means re-rendering the bed files). Listen (Level 24's first 20 s as the game mixes them, recorded from the game's own output, the bed europa-under with track 10, the radio and the guns; each player another run of the same seconds): a as it is, +6 dB, +9.1 dB. Measured: in the level's first seconds, before the music and the guns, the bed's low band rises 4.8 dB (b) and 7.6 dB (c); from about t=5 on the music and the fights cover it and the mix changes by under 0.5 dB. Recommendation: a (0.70)

Weak spots (Claude) under the music and the guns even +9.1 dB is barely heard: a bed is heard in the quiet stretches (a level's start, the pauses before a boss, the arenas' gaps), not in a fight

Answer as recommended a
