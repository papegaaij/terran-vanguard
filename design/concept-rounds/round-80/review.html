Concept round 80: record of the decisions

Level 42 and the end of Act 6 are built (M10 part H, branch m10-act-6): the run through Callisto's orbit past the burning home fleet, the Ascendant's two-phase fight, the hull breaking along its jagged fracture, Vorne's lifeboat escaping, the closing lines, the debrief, the act's outro and the release's end naming Act VII. Asked here, by watching and listening: the whole boss fight and the whole level with the act's end, recorded in real time with sound; part H's art as it looks in the game; the boss's sounds and the escape sting; Vorne's lines; two Act 6 image sets not shown before; and round 79's two follow-ups. The recommendation is pre-selected on every row.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.
