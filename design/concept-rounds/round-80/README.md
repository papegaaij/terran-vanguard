# Concept round 80 — M10 part H "Level 42 Ascendant" and the end of Act 6

## Summary

Level 42 and the end of Act 6 are built (M10 part H, branch `m10-act-6`): the run through Callisto's orbit past the
burning home fleet, the Ascendant's two-phase fight, the hull breaking along its jagged fracture, Vorne's lifeboat
escaping, the closing lines, the debrief, the act's outro and the release's end naming Act VII. Asked here, by watching
and listening: the whole boss fight and the whole level with the act's end, recorded in real time with sound; part H's
art as it looks in the game; the boss's sounds and the escape sting; Vorne's lines; two Act 6 image sets not shown
before; and round 79's two follow-ups. The recommendation is pre-selected on every row.
