Concept round 57: record of the decisions

The review round of M8 part G Level 27 (roadmap), built and flown: Level 27 "Hydrothermal Vents", the vent field past the black water, lit by its own vents: the heat plumes (a mouth glowing amber for 1.4 s, then a 1.6 s eruption that burns the ship and the swimmers and bends every shot crossing it by 25°), the Depth Hunter's grey columns rising from below and firing homing torpedoes up the screen (shoot them, fly off their line or fire the Decoy Flares), the Bulwark loaner flying Rook's slot, the hull-code wreck (HDX-417) with Varga's scan and Vorne's motif alone, the four relay beacons on the ridge as the secondary (3 of 4), the Thera Deep hauler Marlin sealed in Vrell resin (the secret) and the hatchery gate's arches at the end. You can play it: the campaign now runs to Level 27 (Level 28 and the act's end are part H). Answer the rows below: Level 26's Rook loss again and the waterline strip from round 56 (accept or redo), the production art with game captures (accept or redo), twelve sounds (a, b or redo) and seven calls the build made (accept or change; the recommendation is pre-selected). The many small rules decided on the way are in the parts' Decisions logs, marked "pending the user's review in round 57" (collected in Level 27's step G5 entry); they are not asked here.

In the game

The art below is already in assets/ (the finals), and the a sound of every pair plays in the game until the pick (heard in the capture videos). Level 27 plays track 11 over the new europa-vents bed (S.1's a): the bed alone in section 1, the theme with the plumes, the motif layer from t=56 and alone over the bed while Varga reads the hull code (t=68–82), the theme going out over the last 6 s. The frame rate of a full medium run on the software renderer (Xvfb, llvmpipe, no capture): 459 fps on average (Level 26: 333); the longest frame (51 ms) is the level's first frame, the first-time loads; no frame of 50 ms or more after it.

Sounds

Every effect is cut from Freesound's public HQ previews (a pick is rebuilt from its original afterwards with the same settings); no source is AI-made (every description checked; passed over: a "simulated" jet burner, designed under-water rumbles and explosions, synthesized hums and sparks, every mud pot and hot spring, all bubbling). The rule for all of them (round 48): real recordings, heavy and big, muffled and pressured under water, no bubbles, nothing that pops. They are levelled beside the files they stand with. Not listened to by Claude: checked by level, peaks, the envelope, the spectrum, a click scan and the loop seams; no recommendation. The a files play in the game now (heard in the capture videos). The scan ping is synthesized (an instrument ping, as UI sounds may be). Each player is labelled with its variant letter. Reused, no row: the Glassfin's shatter for a torpedo shot down, the under-water explosion family (the Hunter's medium, the beacons' and the Marlin's rungs), the Decoy Flares' launch. Not made: the Hunter's rise (call C.7). Two variants use a 1981 tape of Iceland's Geysir with tourists on it (S.1 b, S.3 a): listen for voices.
