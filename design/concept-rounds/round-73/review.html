Concept round 73: record of the decisions

Open. M10 part A is built (branch m10-act-6, local commits through fb579041; the full ./gradlew check green on 2026-10-31, Acts 1–5's golden flights and replays unchanged): the mirror's reflect, regeneration, the Graft Turret's regrowth, the death-burst on contact, the Act 6 shields, lightning strikes, dry eruptions, foreground banks, the pylon dome and gates, the three-way prey link, the IFF spoofer, the Plasma Arc, the Reflex Shield, Composite V and the Mk V+ "Crucible", flown on fixtures (no Act 6 level exists yet: Level 36 is part B). This round asks, in your order: the mirror and dome sounds (QA-1), the lightning strike and the Reflex bolt (QA-2, QA-3) and a new question, explosions over Level 36's bright bands; then part A's production art to confirm with the first game captures, the round-72 follow-ups (the Ascendant's jagged break, the falling city, Aurelia Traffic's "lift", Vorne's shout) and last the important calls the steps made, each with its recommendation. The recommendation is pre-selected on every row; leave a row alone to keep it.

The captures come from a scratch capture rig (a stand-in Level 36 over ART-B's real Level 36 tiles and over round 72's mood grade, stand-in sprites for the new units; the rig). Found while capturing: the mirror's code-drawn plate is pale on the cream (production art is part C's); the banks are flat pale veils until part B draws ART-B's bank pieces; the arc's violet-pink fringe and the bounced orbs' magenta read apart by shape and brightness, though the hues are close. Gaps noted for later parts: homing missiles do not seek prey (the doc does not say they should; not built); Level 39's vent "ripple" is lost after the first eruption (each vent draws its own period; part E).

Decided as trivia, not asked (m10a-gaps §4): the mirror's facing is its own state; a bounce mirrors about that facing at 110 px/s (off the bullet-speed lever) and a homing missile bounces as a bullet; lightning kills pay as a special's; eruption rocks are their own pool, fanned evenly, never homed on; a dome covers by unit centre each step; the prey link is a wave's hunts: <tag>; the spoofer delays a stored aim; several gates per level; banks are rectangles drifting with high-air; shields.heavy from Level 36; the Crucible at index 5; Composite V in the shop from Level 38; the Harrow's hard ring speed as written.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.
