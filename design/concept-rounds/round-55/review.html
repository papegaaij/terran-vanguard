Concept round 55: record of the decisions

The review round of M8 part F Level 26 (roadmap), built and flown: Level 26 "Blackwater", the black water beyond the trench, where only the ship's headlights and whatever glows show anything: the last lamps going out behind the ship (the ambient 20 → 6 %), the Glow Angler's teal lure and its lunge, the Driftjellies and Eel Swarms by their glow alone, the fault scarp with its Siren nests, the Sonar Pulse lighting everything for 6 s, the Choir's first whispered words with the static before them, the farm barge's warm lamp (the secret) and Rook's loss: three torpedo trails from below, the hits, his lamp spinning down, the silence and the vents' warm pad; the debrief names him ROOK MIA. Answer the rows below: Level 25's redo from round 54 (accept or redo), the production art (accept or redo), the sounds and the warm pad (a, b or redo) and seven calls the build made (accept or change; the recommendation is pre-selected). The many small rules decided on the way are in the parts' Decisions logs, marked "pending the user's review in round 55"; they are not asked here.

Next, before part G: the under-water weapons rework (AW) from your playtest of Levels 22–25 is decided and is built right after this round, before Level 27 (energy weapons nearly useless under water, the Harpoon tuned for swarms, the Depth Charges thrown forward, the Act 4 briefing's warning, no selling in the plan); nothing of it is in this build.

In the game

The art below is already in assets/ (the finals), and the a sound of every pair plays in the game until the pick (heard in the capture videos), the Choir's static a and the warm pad a included. Level 26 plays track 10, the Act 4 A theme: the full theme with the lamps, its dark stem alone from t=8–18, thinning at t=150, out at t=160 for the loss's silence, the warm pad from t=168; over the europa-black bed (S.9's a) with europa-trench over the scarp. The frame rate of a full medium run on the software renderer (Xvfb, llvmpipe, no capture): 333 fps on average (Level 25: 190 before LX); the longest frame (110 ms) is the level's first frame, the first-time loads; no frame of 50 ms or more after it.

Sounds

Every effect is cut from Freesound's public HQ previews (a pick is rebuilt from its original afterwards with the same settings); no source is AI-made (every description checked). The rule for all of them (round 48): real recordings, heavy and big, muffled and pressured under water, no bubbles, nothing that pops. They are levelled beside the files they stand with. Not listened to by Claude: checked by level, peaks, the envelope, the spectrum and the loop seams; no recommendation. The a files play in the game now (heard in the capture videos). The warm pad is synthesized music (the music tool). Each player is labelled with its variant letter. Reused, no row: the Angler's ring (the enemy small shot), the Driftjelly's pulse, the Siren's ring and europa-trench over the scarp.
