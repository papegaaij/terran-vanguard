# Concept round 55 — M8 part F, Level 26 Blackwater

## Summary

The review round of M8 part F *Level 26* ([roadmap](../../tech/roadmap/README.md#m8-parts)), built and flown:
[Level 26 "Blackwater"](../../campaign/act-4-deep-water/level-26-blackwater/README.md), the black water beyond the
trench, where only the ship's headlights and whatever glows show anything: the last lamps going out behind the ship
(the ambient 20 → 6 %), the [Glow Angler](../../enemies/naval/glow-angler/README.md)'s teal lure and its lunge, the
Driftjellies and Eel Swarms by their glow alone, the fault scarp with its Siren nests, the Sonar Pulse lighting
everything for 6 s, the Choir's first whispered words with the static before them, the farm barge's warm lamp (the
secret) and **Rook's loss**: three torpedo trails from below, the hits, his lamp spinning down, the silence and the
vents' warm pad; the debrief names him `ROOK  MIA`. Answer the rows below: Level 25's redo from round 54 (accept or
redo), the production art (accept or redo), the sounds and the warm pad (a, b or redo) and seven calls the build made
(accept or change; the recommendation is pre-selected). The many small rules decided on the way are in the parts'
*Decisions* logs, marked "pending the user's review in round 55"; they are not asked here.

**Next, before part G:** the under-water weapons rework (AW) from your playtest of Levels 22–25 is decided and is built
right after this round, before Level 27 (energy weapons nearly useless under water, the Harpoon tuned for swarms, the
Depth Charges thrown forward, the Act 4 briefing's warning, no selling in the plan); nothing of it is in this build.

balance plan's Level 26 fit: Harpoon Torpedoes L4, Depth Charges L3, two Micro-Missile Pods L3, the Pressure hull, the
Sonar Pulse pressed at t≈64 and t≈110, Rook along); their PNGs are frames of those runs side by side, the time in the
level in each row's text. The Angler's capture comes from a run with the guns held (so the lunges are not shot first).

recommendation. The **a** files play in the game now (heard in the capture videos). The warm pad is synthesized music
(the music tool). Each player is labelled with its variant letter. Reused, no row: the Angler's ring (the enemy small
shot), the Driftjelly's pulse, the Siren's ring and `europa-trench` over the scarp.
