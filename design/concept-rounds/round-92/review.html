Concept round 92: record of the decisions

M11 part E was built (branch m11-act-7, uncommitted; the full ./gradlew check green on 2026-11-07, Acts 1–6's golden flights and replays unchanged): Level 47 "Seraph Gauntlet" with the Choir Seraphs jumping in by their rings, mirroring the ship in pairs and folding away, the Heralds over three of the pairs, the inner-sphere backdrop and the core's light, the memory pearl, track 17 by section and the radio voiced. This round asks, in this order: the inner sphere's ambience bed (a/b), track 17 in place, part E's art to confirm, the level in play from game captures (and round 91's crack cue again, fixed), and the important calls, each with its recommendation. The recommendation is pre-selected on every row; leave a row alone to keep it. The real-time captures have the game's sound; the two flown without firing are silent.

Decided as trivia, not asked: the Herald hangs a little closer over its pair so its ring reaches the upper Seraph; the Herald leaves as its pair starts to leave, so a rising Seraph never touches it; a Seraph's ring ends at once when it jumps away; a pair's two rows are moved together so they never overlap; easy's single Seraphs and hard's extra pair and Harrow streams are separate waves per difficulty, the Harrows 1 s later so their warnings never meet a pair's arrival; the small enemies keep off the centre while a pair is on the screen; Varga's briefing page was shortened to fit one screen, the meaning kept; the memory pearl is a tumbling stone shard that drifts down the field; Level 47's money scale is 0.17.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.
