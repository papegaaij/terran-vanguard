# Concept round 75 — M10 part C "Level 37 Fall of Aurelia"

## Summary

**Closed 2026-11-01**: the answers are in the Answer and Outcome columns and in the *Decisions* entry (everything as recommended except **S.1 b**, **S.5 b**, **S.8 b**, **V.4 b** and **F.6 redo**; F.3 accepted with a note that changed the shuttles' path). M10 part C was built on the branch `m10-act-6` (local commits through `0c5ab1e5`; the full `./gradlew check`
green on 2026-11-01 (4,961 tests) in step C5, Acts 1–5's golden flights and replays unchanged): Level 37 "Fall of Aurelia" plays from its
briefing to the release's end, with the Mirror Interceptor and the Graft Turret in a level, the six Halcyon shuttles
in Aurelia's livery as the `SHUTTLES` secondary (marked friendly: the banner and the brackets), the Vrell turn at t=100
(the Choir's line, the growth's burst over the decks, Vorne's motif cut, the four prey links from t=103), the falling
city (t=160–200: the deck breaking into two layers along a jagged cut), track 15 "Storm Wall" from the turn and its
`fall` stem, the `aurelia` bed and its burning layer, and the level's voices. This round asks, in this order: part C's
production **art to confirm** beside the first Level 37 captures, the level's **sounds** and track 15, its
**voices**, four **Level 37 questions** (the briefing pages' length, the hard shuttles, the crowded radio, the
Choir's static) and last the **important calls** the steps made, each with its recommendation. The recommendation is
pre-selected on every row; leave a row alone to keep it.

The captures are the game's own (the [recipe](../../campaign/act-6-jovian-storm/level-37-fall-of-aurelia/concept/prompts.md#level-37-capture-r75-a)):
the balance plan's Level 37 fit on medium, the ship invulnerable; a parked run (the ship where it launches, the beam
up the middle), a mirror window (the ship steered under the left t=38 mirror), a hunt window (steered off the middle so
the t=116 Gunship lives for the snake) and a spread run (the Scatter Vulcan in front, for the bounces). Found while
capturing: with the ship in the middle its beam kills the t=116 Gunship before the t=118 snake reaches it; the beam and
the Tesla pod together break a mirror in about 0.6 s, so its heat stages flash by; the parked ship never shoots the
locker. From the full check: the autopilot meets the shuttles in every easy and medium run, in none of six on hard (L.2).

Decided as trivia, not asked (m10c-gaps §4): the shuttles are Level 10's air allies (enemy fire hurts them) marked
with the brackets and the banner, not `neutral` units; the secondary fails at the (6 − target + 1)-th loss (medium the
2nd, easy the 3rd, hard the 1st); a hunting snake leaves its path for its prey from the turn and, with no prey left,
flies on toward the ship's last bearing and out; the falling halves are authored art swapped in for the tiles at
t=160; the darker grade is the turn's atmosphere stretch over 4 s plus the growth overlay; the mirrors' station heights
are re-spaced for the 80 px gap and the rear lanes split the field evenly; the motif stops at t=100 under the Choir's
static.
