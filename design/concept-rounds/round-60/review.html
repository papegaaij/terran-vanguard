Concept round 60: record of the decisions

Act 5 (Levels 29-35, the asteroid belt and Ceres Hub, the act) has been designed on branch m9-act-5: the act, its seven levels, the Iron Sovereign, the new units, the mining tug and the systems are written as draft documents from the recommended answers to the questions below (you asked for that), and nothing is built yet: no data.yaml, no code, until you have answered this round. The art, the music and the sounds are mockups for you to choose from. The refactoring before Act 5 (the content validator split by concern, the enemy data by family, the golden flights, the spec builders, the set-piece and renderer split) is done and pushed; it needs no answer.

Answer by section, in this order:

  • Questions Q.1-Q.12: the twelve design questions, each with its options, the recommendation pre-selected and what was drafted (so far the recommendation in every case).
  • Judgement calls J.1-J.12: choices the drafting made where the questions left a gap, each with what it does and the alternative; the recommendation is the draft.
  • Art A.1-A.23: the units, the boss, the belt's mood, the tug, Vorne's portraits and the speakers, each a or b.
  • Music M.1-M.2, sounds S.1-S.19, Vorne's voice V.1: pick a or b by how each sounds; every row says where and when it plays and what it should sound like.
  • Documents D.1-D.19: the drafts to approve, one row each.

Rows left unanswered keep the recommendation (the draft). Only you approve a document; nothing here sets design: approved by itself.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.

The generator drain (L33)
