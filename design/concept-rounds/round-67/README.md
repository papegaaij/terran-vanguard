# Concept round 67 — M9 part G, Level 34 "Salvage Run"

## Summary

**Partly answered 2026-10-29, still open**: the answered rows are filled in; G.1, G.2, G.3 and S.4 are **re-shown** after a redo (step R67REDO): G.1's b and c and G.2's b rendered as concept mockups beside a, G.3 as four short game clips on the tanks, S.4's groans re-levelled louder with solo and in-mix files.

The review round of M9 part G ([roadmap](../../tech/roadmap/README.md#m9-parts)), now **built**: [Level
34](../../campaign/act-5-the-belt/level-34-salvage-run/README.md) flies end to end on every difficulty (the autopilot wins
every flight with and without Rook, `CONTAINERS` met): the ship graveyard of CDF and Helix hulls, the
[Warden Tank](../../enemies/ground/warden-tank/README.md) back in the Ascendancy's livery on the hulls' walkways and keel
roads, three mining charges beside the roads, six cargo containers with their salvage (`CONTAINERS n / 6`), three hidden
crates (the *Vigil*, the *Sable Wren*, *Old Marta*), the CDF beacon's armour patch, and the freighter's hold: four
clamps shot loose, the Helix core rising out of the hold and collected by flying through it (`HELIX CORE SALVAGED`; the
Mk V comes with the win). Track 12's base stem at −3 dB with Vorne's motif once over the hold, the `wreck-groans` layer
over `belt-open`, 16 voiced lines. The release now ends after Level 34's win ("LEVEL 35 COMING SOON").

The rows ask: part G's production art with game captures (accept or redo; **pick a, b or c** for the livery on the
Helix hulls, QG-1, and **a or b** for the core's salvage, QG-2), four sounds (a or b; **a** plays in the game until you
pick), and six calls the build made (the recommendation is pre-selected), the first an **important concept: the level's
density (C.1)**. Decided as trivia and only logged (in the parts' *Decisions*): the crate's geometry and frame rates, the
`HELIX CORE SALVAGED` event-prompt line, the `CONTAINERS n / 6` tag, the intel's `OBJECTIVE: HELIX CORE` tag and the
gold underlines for the crates' sections, the tank's liveried portrait shown first, Level 32's conveyor stem at 56–72 and
116–132 (round 65 M.1), the core's hum at the collection only (the rise carried by the motif), the groans as an ambience
layer, the autopilot finding 1–3 of the 3 hidden crates a run (≈ 1.9, fewer on hard).

## Review

The review page is built from this file by `build/round67-tools/build_review.py` (untracked); the board is
[index.html](index.html) (`python3 tools/concept/board.py 67`). The art generators are `tools/art/backdrop_l34.py`,
`tools/art/belt_units_g.py`, `tools/art/warden_tank.py --livery asc`, `tools/art/intel.py` and
`tools/art/briefing_images.py`; the sounds `tools/concept/audio/sfx_r67.py` (AUD-G). The captures are recordings of the
game with its own sound (Xvfb, `--bench`, the plan's Level 34 fit, Rook in his slot, the ship steered by timed key
presses; the [recipe](../../campaign/act-5-the-belt/level-34-salvage-run/concept/prompts.md#level-34-capture-r67-a)); their
PNGs are frames of the same recordings, labelled with the level time.

Runs of Level 34 at medium with `--invulnerable`: the ship under each container, charge, secret and the hold's clamps as
they come (the picket charge blown as a convoy passes), the same with each opened well down the screen (their shut looks
first) through the debrief, one with the ship away from the *Vigil*'s deck (its container and the beacon unshot), one
aborted to the hangar at sensor L3 ([the intel](../../ui/hangar/concept/intel-capture-r67-a.png)), and one to the
release's end (the debrief, then "ACTS I-IV COMPLETE", "ACT V - THE BELT. LEVEL 35 COMING SOON.":
[capture](../../campaign/act-5-the-belt/concept/release-end-capture-r67-a.png); built without step H2's Level 35 data,
which the game otherwise flies on into). The video ([video with sound](../../campaign/act-5-the-belt/level-34-salvage-run/concept/level-34-capture-r67-a.mp4)) has the groans under `belt-open` with a container's burst, a
charge taking a tank, and the hold with the motif's window (t=106–124), the four clamps and the core's hum.

Found in the captures, for the next game or content step (not asked): the intel names no faction for the tank (the
unit's `faction` is loaded, the game does not read it; the Warden Tank's item stays open); the wrecked containers are
black holes in the deck (G.5); the beam opens a container or a secret's trigger as it comes over the top edge when the
ship is under it; the containers' salvage and the crates drift down slowly, so a ship kept low collects few of them
(one run `SALVAGE 0`, the other four of six); the four clamps die within about half a second under the plan's beam, so
their sounds come together (S.2's cap of two at once matters).
