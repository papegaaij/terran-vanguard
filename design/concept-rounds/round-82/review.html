Concept round 82: record of the decisions

Open since 2026-11-01. The databank is the feature the user asked for on 2026-11-01: an encyclopaedia of enemies, bosses, weapons, ship parts and key concepts with pictures, numbers and weapon advice, opened from the hangar and the main menu (draft, step DB0, mockups in its concept/). Already decided with the user and not asked again: entries exist only once met (enemies and bosses) or owned or offered (gear), and nothing of a later act is named; an enemy's or boss's detail follows the sensor suite the campaign owns now (none shows the name and a silhouette, L1 to L3 fill in more; buying a better suite fills every met entry at once); the player's own gear is always shown in full; it is reachable from the hangar and the main menu; it is designed and built now, beside Act 6. This round asks for the design to confirm, the layout, and the seven open questions of the draft, each with its recommendation pre-selected: leave a row alone to keep it. The build (steps DB-TXT, DB-ART, DB1-DB3) follows the answers.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.

Q.1
