# Concept round 82 — the databank

## Summary

Open since 2026-11-01. The databank is the feature the user asked for on 2026-11-01: an encyclopaedia of enemies, bosses,
weapons, ship parts and key concepts with pictures, numbers and weapon advice, opened from the hangar and the main menu
([draft](../../ui/databank/README.md), step DB0, mockups in its [concept/](../../ui/databank/concept/prompts.md)). Already
decided with the user and not asked again: entries exist only once **met** (enemies and bosses) or **owned or offered**
(gear), and nothing of a later act is named; an enemy's or boss's detail follows the **sensor suite the campaign owns now**
(none shows the name and a silhouette, L1 to L3 fill in more; buying a better suite fills every met entry at once); the
player's own gear is always shown in full; it is reachable from the hangar and the main menu; it is designed and built
now, beside Act 6. This round asks for the design to confirm, the layout, and the seven open questions of the draft, each
with its recommendation pre-selected: leave a row alone to keep it. The build (steps DB-TXT, DB-ART, DB1-DB3) follows the
answers.

| Q.1 | the unknown counter's reach | **pick one**. What the `n UNKNOWN` count and the dimmed `???` rows mean. **Every act reached:** the unknown entries of the whole current act and the earlier ones (in Act VI before Level 40 the rows `ACT VI ???` stand for Levels 40-42's units, never a later act). **Only units missed in levels already flown:** no hint at all of what is ahead in the act, and a count that only a replay can bring down. | — | you see how much of the act's roster is still to come, which invites replays to fill the page; it also tells you roughly how many new units the next levels bring (not what they are); the alternative never hints, but the count then stays at 0 for most players | **every act reached** / only missed in levels flown | open |
| Q.2 | the level whose numbers an enemy entry shows | **pick one**. HP, shield, heat and bounty are computed for one level and difficulty, named beside the number (`120  AT L37, MEDIUM`). **Last level met:** what you faced most recently. **First level met:** the debut. **The next level when it flies there:** the number you are about to face. | — | HP grows by act, so a unit met in Act V reads higher after Act VI; last-met is true to what you fought and needs nothing about levels ahead; the next-level option needs the next level's waves, a spoiler the hangar intel already gives at sensor L2 | **last level met** / first level met / next level where it flies | open |
| Q.3 | one databank per campaign or per player | **pick one**. **Per campaign:** the record lives in the save, like the sensor suite that unlocks its detail; a new game starts with an empty databank. **Per player:** a profile record beside the settings that survives a new game. | — | per campaign cannot show a new game what a past one met (no spoilers, and the suite is per save anyway); per player would show a second campaign's player units it has never met and break the 'only what the campaign knows' rule | **per campaign** / per player | open |
| Q.4 | a stored sensor suite counts | **pick one**. The suite level the databank uses. **A stored suite counts:** the best suite the campaign owns, fitted or in storage (a databank is read at home, not in flight). **Only a fitted one:** as the hangar's intel panel, which asks for the suite on the ship. | — | with the first you never have to refit a suite to read a page, and buying one fills every entry at once as decided; the two screens then disagree (the intel panel shows less than the databank for a stored suite), a small oddity the databank's own sensor entry explains | **stored counts** / only fitted | open |
| Q.5 | replays count | **pick one**.
