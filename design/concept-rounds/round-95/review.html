Concept round 95: record of the decisions

M11 part H was built (branch m11-act-7, uncommitted; the full ./gradlew check green on 2026-11-08, Acts 1–6's golden flights and replays unchanged): Level 50 "Choir Heart", the campaign's last level: the hive core's approach, the Choir Heart built to your boss recipe (each membrane and node its own gun, silent when destroyed), the gate closing behind the wing, the race home and the near side, and the campaign's end screens. This round asks, in this order: the part's art, the sounds and track 20 in place, the voices (the Heart's five lines, the ending pages), the level, the whole Heart fight, the gate scene and the end screens in play, three boss calls and the important calls, each with its recommendation. The recommendation is pre-selected on every row; leave a row alone to keep it. Round 94 comes first: it has the echoes and the reworked Chimera.

Decided as trivia, not asked: the walls are screen-space terrain owned by the Heart, so everything that stops at rock stops at them; the nodes ride their half-wall's inner face and a cut node's half retreats; the darkness is a look only; a checkpoint restores the Heart at the start of its phase; the Heart's bounty is paid at the kill, as every boss's; the race's chambers are a dedicated grey strip; the K+32 radio lines have two versions (with and without Rook); the scene runs on the level clock after the arena; the Heart's death has no hang and a flash 3.5 s after the kill; the heartbeat is an interval attack of its phase; the seed vault is the draft's "memory organ"; the level's secondary target is 382 kills (65 % of 587), not the 219 the draft said; the two Seraph pairs and the Heralds were moved a little so their rules hold; the money scale is 0.20.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.
