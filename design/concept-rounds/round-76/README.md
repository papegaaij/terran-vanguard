# Concept round 76 — M10 part D "Level 38 Lightning Belt"

## Summary

**Closed 2026-11-01**: the answers are in the Answer and Outcome columns and in the *Decisions* entry (every row as recommended, D.1 approved, L.1 b: the canister has no latch). M10 part D was built on the branch `m10-act-6` (local commits through `01a9f0ce`; the full `./gradlew check`
on 2026-11-01 in step D5: 5,101 tests, every one green but Level 39's three known reds that step E4 fixes —
`RadioTimelineLevelsTest`, `Level14SoundsTest` and `BackdropAssetsTest`, Level 39's data being in and its game side
not —; Acts 1–5's golden flights and replays unchanged): Level 38 "Lightning Belt" plays from its briefing to the
release's end, with the Harrow (chase, lock tell, a straight ram out through the far edge, its ring of 8 on every death),
26 lightning strikes at medium with six lures on the Harrow waves (13 on easy, eight tracking columns on hard), the
Buzzsaw Drones' return, waves from all four edges with their warnings, the two hunts, the gust spells, the
`HARROWS n / 23` kill ratio judged at the end, Aurelia's cargo pod (the secret) and the CDF canister on its parachute,
the storm-layer backdrop from Aurelia's wreck to Io's disc, track 15 with the `jovian-sky` bed raised in the storm, and
the level's voices. This round asks, in this order: part D's production **art to confirm** beside the first Level 38
captures, the Harrow's two new **sounds**, the level's **voices**, the Buzzsaw's Act 6 return to **approve** (QD-1),
three **Level 38 questions** (the canister, the caps with nothing shot, Rook's armour) and last the **important calls**
the steps made, each with its recommendation. The recommendation is pre-selected on every row; leave a row alone to
keep it.

The captures are the game's own (the [recipe](../../campaign/act-6-jovian-storm/level-38-lightning-belt/concept/prompts.md#level-38-capture-r76-a)):
the balance plan's Level 38 fit on medium (Level 37's: the Ion Beam, the Tesla Coil Pod, a Micro-missile Pod, the Swarm
Tail, Rook with his Autocannon), the ship invulnerable; a parked run (the ship where it launches; the gusts at t=80/86
push it to the left edge, where it stays), a cells window (steered left so the beam leaves the t=62 Buzzsaw circle), a
canister run (the Scatter Vulcan in front, steered under the canister) and a hard run for a tracking column. Found while
capturing: with the plan's beam the canister drifts out at the bottom unshot (the beam passes debris); the Scatter
Vulcan breaks its latch in about half a second; the parked run breaks the cargo pod with its own fire and Rook's just
before the t=122.5 column would have opened it.

Decided as trivia, not asked (m10d-gaps §4): the secondary's key is the Implementation's `kill_ratio` with `enemy:
harrow` (not `escapes`); the Harrow caps are authored and measured, not a simulation rule; the t=159.5 Skitters fly a
rear snake on its path and the t=74 Lampreys the built side swarm; each lure's column x sits on its wave's flown chase
lane; the walls' inner lightning is presentation only; the cargo pod's outline is a 0.5 s look on a lightning flash.
