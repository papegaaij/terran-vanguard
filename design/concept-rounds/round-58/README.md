# Concept round 58 — M8 part H, Level 28 Abyssal Maw and the end of Act 4

## Summary

The review round of M8 part H *Level 28 + act end* ([roadmap](../../tech/roadmap/README.md#m8-parts)), built and
flown: [Level 28 "Abyssal Maw"](../../campaign/act-4-deep-water/level-28-abyssal-maw/README.md), the Act 4 finale.
Through the hatchery gate into the Vrell nursery (the Depth Hunters guarding the gate beside the Vrell, the egg-rack
galleries, the Sirens on the gallery walls, the spawning cavern with the UTC recon sub *Pelagic-3*, the antechamber
where the Choir sings), then the act boss on its halted arena: the
[Abyssal Maw](../../enemies/bosses/abyssal-maw/README.md) coiled round the vent (the gill sacs and the throat, the
fan walls with their gaps, the coil's sweeps, one dive into the dark and the lunge up a column, the Helix implants
tearing open in phase 3, the death). Then **the reveal**: the climb up the drill shaft, the breakout through the ice
into the air, two Ascendancy cruisers in black and gold rising beside the Vrell carriers with `HDX-417` on their bows,
a 35 s run under their broadsides, flak and point defence, the orbit; the debrief with the act summary, the Act 4
outro and the release's end card naming Act V. **You can play it through to the release's end**: the campaign now
runs from Level 01 to the end of Act 4 (part I, the close-out, follows). Answer the rows below: the production art
with game captures (accept or redo), nineteen sounds (a, b or redo) and track 30, Q.1 the Maw's music and eight calls
the build made (the recommendation pre-selected). The many small rules decided on the way are in the parts'
*Decisions* logs, marked "pending the user's review in round 58" (collected in
[Level 28's](../../campaign/act-4-deep-water/level-28-abyssal-maw/README.md#decisions) step H7 entry); they are not
asked here.

## Sounds

Every effect is cut from Freesound's public HQ previews (a pick is rebuilt from its original afterwards with the same
settings); no source is AI-made or a designed effect (every description read). The rule for all of them (round 48):
real recordings, heavy and big, muffled and pressured under water, **no bubbles, nothing that pops**; above the ice
(the breakout and the cruisers: the medium is air again) the layers stay open. Levelled beside the Act 4 files they
stand with. Not listened to by Claude: checked by level, peaks, the envelope, the spectrum, a click scan and the loop
seams; no recommendation. The **a** files play in the game now (heard in the capture videos). Each player is labelled
with its variant letter. Reused, no row: the under-water explosion family (the Maw's death adds its own boom), the
*Marlin*'s crack for *Pelagic-3*, `europa-ice` above the ice, the Depth Hunters' sounds, the boss warning (track 22).
Decided by the sound step (no question): the coil sweep as two files (its tell's groan and the swing, since the tell's
length varies by difficulty), the implant as two (the spark tell ending in the plate's clunk; one zap per discharge),
the antechamber's rumble as its own bed over the hatchery's, the shaft's climb one 10 s sound.
