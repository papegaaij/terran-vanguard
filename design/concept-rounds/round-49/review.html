Dome hit (Level 17), third try. It plays at every enemy shot that hits one of Level 17's colony greenhouse domes (Sfx.DOME_HIT): often, several in a row, so it is at most 1.2 s and must not tire. The dome breaking is a separate sound (no shatter here). It should sound like a glass dome tens of metres across, the size of a stadium roof, being hit: both sharp and huge, a hard bright strike and then the big structure answering (a long low glassy resonance or boom, cracks running away across a large surface like a frozen lake's ice), heard from a distance. Your verdicts so far: round 46 (two thuds, rejected, playable below): "Neither sound like glass being hit. Glass being hit is a sharp sound, not a thud." Round 47 (a sharp hit on a pane plus a glass crack; a tapped window plus a cooling-glass crack, both rejected, playable below): "These are too small. a is a bottle cracking, b is nothing like glass breaking. A dome is a very large structure." So each variant below is built to be both sharp and huge. a: a steel hammer striking a big glass pane on a wall (loud and bright, not breaking) and, right under it, a frozen lake's ice cracking: one deep crack, then a run of smaller cracks racing away across the sheet, each with the ice's falling 'zing', slowed to 0.8 so the sheet sounds bigger. Sharp, glassy and wide; the brightest of the three, 1.2 s (CC0). b: the same hammer on a glass pane, slowed to 0.85 (heavier), over a struck glass table's ringing (a contact-mic recording) slowed to 0.5, an octave down, so a large glass body hums on after the hit; both played into a real cathedral (convolved with its recorded impulse response), so it rings out in a huge space as if heard from far below. A bright 'clank' with a long low glassy 'hmmm'; no ice, the most 'glass in a big hall', 1.2 s (CC0, CC-BY 4.0). c: a rock slammed onto a frozen lake (a hard icy crack with the ice's 'zing') on a deep crack and boom of the lake's thick ice slowed to 0.85, and from 0.25 s a tall steel door's low groan under it, quiet, like the dome's frame taking the blow. Harder and darker than a, no glass pane; the most 'huge' (most of its energy is low), the least bright, 1.2 s (CC0, CC-BY 4.0). No recommendation (I cannot listen).
