# Terran Vanguard @tag@ — the whole campaign

A late-90s style vertical shoot'em up set in 2185. This release holds the whole campaign: seven acts, fifty levels on easy, medium or hard, from Earth orbit and Luna (Act 1, First Contact) over Earth's cities, oceans and arctic (Act 2, Homefront), the Mars colonies (Act 3, Red Dust), Jupiter's moon Europa (Act 4, Deep Water), the asteroid belt (Act 5, The Belt) and Jupiter and its moons (Act 6, Jovian Storm) through the Tether Gate beyond Neptune into Vrell space (Act 7, Beyond the Gate), and the ending. Each act has its briefings, radio chatter and outro, the shop and the hangar between the levels, and a boss at its end: the Brood Carrier, the Siege Spire, the Dust Colossus, the Abyssal Maw, the Iron Sovereign, the Ascendant and the Choir Heart. From Act 2 on Rook flies with you as your wingman.

New in this release, Act 7: eight levels beyond the gate, from the Gate Wardens at the ring's mouth through distorted space, where the scroll turns and reverses, a living reef of spawning organs and a dead Vrell fleet hollowed from the inside, the gauntlet of the Choir's Seraphs and the citadel where Vorne ends fused with his lifeboat (Vorne's Chimera), to the Choir's memory, where the Brood Carrier, the Dust Colossus and the Iron Sovereign return as echoes in a boss rush, and the hive core, where the Choir Heart falls and the closing gate is raced. New in the shop: the Choir Resonator, a front gun grown from a Gate Warden's eye, the Bastion Shield, the Vrell Swarm Call and the Mk VI "Choir Core" generator. After Level 50 come the ending, the credits and the end card; every mission then stays open for replay in Missions. A save from v0.4 carries on: Continue opens Act 7's title card and intro once, then Level 43's briefing.

## The game is not signed

  • Windows: SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway (only on the first start).
  • macOS: the first start is refused ("cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified"). Control-click the app, choose Open and confirm, or choose Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security. If macOS says the app is damaged, run once in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/path/to/Terran Vanguard.app".
  • Linux: if the game does not start, make it executable: chmod +x terran-vanguard.

## Requirements

A 64-bit system with OpenGL 3.2 or newer (any graphics chip from the last ten years); keyboard or gamepad. The game starts in borderless full screen; Alt+Enter or F11 switch to a window.
