# Concept round 78 — M10 part F "Level 40 Honour Guard"

## Summary

**Closed 2026-11-01**: the answers are in the Answer and Outcome columns and in the *Decisions* entry (everything as
recommended except **S.5, S.7, S.8 and S.9 b**, **V.2 keep** and **B.1 change**). **The user's feedback on the round
itself:** it asked too many trivial questions, showed unreadable blobs of numbers and its captures were inaccurate; from
now on a round asks only what the user can judge by looking or listening, each question short with a rendering of the
difference, and a boss question comes with a full real-time recording of the fight. M10 part F was built on the branch `m10-act-6` (local commits through `24fe6985`; the
full `./gradlew check` on 2026-11-01 in step F5 on a snapshot of it with `game/` as committed: **5,319 tests, every one
green**; Acts 1–5's golden flights and replays unchanged): Level 40 "Honour Guard" plays from its briefing to the
release's end: the Callisto approach through Vorne's pickets (Talon wings, two of them rising from the rear, Chimera
swarms, Gunships and their Darts, Mirror pincers and rear ambushes, the Harrow cross of 8, Hornets, the Vrell hunting
two Talon waves), the duel with the [Honour Guard](../../enemies/bosses/honour-guard/README.md) at t=102 (three aces
joining one by one, each with its own entry tell, then flying together under the conductor; one segmented bar), the
run-out over Callisto's ring to the citadel's outer line, Sentry Two's recorder (the secret), track 32 "Three Aces" for
the duel and the level's voices. This round asks, in this order: part F's production **art to confirm** beside the first
Level 40 captures, the duel's and the level's **sounds** and track 32, the **voices**, the **boss fight** measured,
four **Level 40 questions** and last the **important calls** the steps made, each with its recommendation. The
recommendation is pre-selected on every row; leave a row alone to keep it.

The captures are the game's own (recipes: [Level 40](../../campaign/act-6-jovian-storm/level-40-honour-guard/concept/prompts.md#level-40-capture-r78-a),
[the duel](../../enemies/bosses/honour-guard/concept/prompts.md#honour-guard-capture-r78-a)): the balance plan's Level 40
fit on medium (the Ion Beam L5, the Tesla Coil Pod L2, a Micro-missile Pod L4, the Swarm Tail L3, the Mk V+ "Crucible"
and the IFF spoofer; Rook with his Autocannon), the ship invulnerable and **parked** where it launches. Found while
capturing: a parked ship takes **4:33** for the duel (the autopilot's 61–71 s): Vesper falls at 68 s, Harker at 98 s,
then Kestrel alone holds his side station outside a parked beam for almost three minutes; the parked beam never frees
the recorder (`SECRETS 0 / 1`); the pincer was not drawn in the 35 s the three flew together, so it has no capture.
The run-out's sound comes from a second, real-time run of the same command laid under the stepped frames.

Decided as trivia, not asked (m10f-gaps §4, built as written): one set piece whose three parts are the aces (an
unjoined ace absent and untouchable); the senior flying survivor speaks the first loss; a retry repeats the duel from
the sting (the built checkpoint); the sniper line tracks the ship until 0.2 s before it fires (Harker only, Level 35's
rail lines unchanged); the ribbon's 8 bullets evenly laid with two 50 px gaps; the kill ratio's requirement 186 of the
285 planned units (the Darts count as kills only); Callisto's growth authored, no runtime scaling; the rear V-wing
flipped as a reversed-scroll wave with its BOTTOM warning; the bounty 15 % of budget(40), a third an ace; Kestrel never
rams on any difficulty.
