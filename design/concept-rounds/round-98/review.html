Concept round 98: record of the decisions

Round 97's six heartbeats were all rejected. S.1: "All bad. These sounds like beats on a drum." S.2: "Also all bad. Doesn't sound like a heart at all". Fair: they were drums with a heart's rhythm. This round has no drum, timpani, taiko or knock left: each of the three beats for the Heart (S.1) is cut from a real recorded heart, slowed for a giant heart, with the lub and the dub kept, and each of the three hive layers (S.2) is the matching beat far off. Pick a, b or c for S.1, then the one matching it for S.2. The pick goes into the game after the choice.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.

The Choir Heart's heartbeat (heart-beat)

pick a, b or c
[The Choir Heart's heartbeat (heart-beat)](../../enemies/bosses/choir-heart/README.md#sounds-brief)
pick a, b or c (recommended: c). Plays: on every pulse of the Heart's light, every 3 s (2 s in phase 3), under the whole final fight over track 20. Should sound like a giant, slow heart beating, a lub-dub, no drum, and you still hear it on a phone. a: a close stethoscope heartbeat, dry, a deep soft lub and a shorter dub. b: an ultrasound heartbeat: two whooshes of rushing blood, a hospital monitor's sound. c: a heartbeat through the chest with a soft wet squelch, rounder and longer than a.

Weak spots (Claude) Not listened to, only measured. All three are real heart recordings with the lub-dub kept, and through a phone-speaker model at their hit they stand level with the music (a −0.5, b 0.0, c +1.2 LU) where the game's is 15 LU under; a may still read as a kick drum, and a and c are limited hard (they may sound a little squashed).
