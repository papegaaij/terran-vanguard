Concept round 63: record of the decisions

The review round of M9 part C (roadmap), now built: Level 30 flies end to end on every difficulty (the autopilot wins 9 of 9 without a retry and meets the convoy every time), the act's one space-only level: the bomb rack reads NO GROUND, Minelayers rise from behind (the BOTTOM warning) and lay lines of proximity mines in clusters of four with one gap, a shot mine sets its cluster off, a blinking mine bursts with its 40 px blast; Gilded Gunships plated in front fire their rings and launch Dart Drone streams; Rail Serpent trains split when a car is shot out; the CONVOY secondary, the dead beacon buoy's secret, Rook back in his slot, "Hollow Rock" and the belt-open bed. The release now ends after Level 30's win ("LEVEL 31 COMING SOON") until part H moves it to Level 35.

The rows ask: the new Act 5 title card (round 62 F.9's redo: three new ideas, pick one), the production art with game captures beside it (accept or redo), ten sounds (a or b; a plays in the game until you pick) and seven calls the build made (the recommendation is pre-selected). Decided as trivia and only logged (in the parts' Decisions): the large rocks at x 60 or 420 only, the Talon's orbit for the convoy escorts, the Mote Swarms 8 strong, a secret's radio line without requires, the failed secondary's line on the first Gunship's escape, the CONVOY label on an escapes objective, the turret cars counted in a chain's HP and bounty sums.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.
