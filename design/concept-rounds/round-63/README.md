# Concept round 63 — M9 part C, Level 30 "Minefield"

## Summary

The review round of M9 part C ([roadmap](../../tech/roadmap/README.md#m9-parts)), now **built**: [Level
30](../../campaign/act-5-the-belt/level-30-minefield/README.md) flies end to end on every difficulty (the autopilot wins
9 of 9 without a retry and meets the convoy every time), the act's one space-only level: the bomb rack reads `NO
GROUND`, [Minelayers](../../enemies/space/minelayer/README.md) rise from behind (the `BOTTOM` warning) and lay lines of
proximity mines in clusters of four with one gap, a shot mine sets its cluster off, a blinking mine bursts with its
40 px blast; [Gilded Gunships](../../enemies/air/gilded-gunship/README.md) plated in front fire their rings and launch
[Dart Drone](../../enemies/air/dart-drone/README.md) streams; [Rail Serpent](../../enemies/air/rail-serpent/README.md)
trains split when a car is shot out; the `CONVOY` secondary, the dead beacon buoy's secret, Rook back in his slot,
"Hollow Rock" and the `belt-open` bed. The release now ends after Level 30's win ("LEVEL 31 COMING SOON") until part H
moves it to Level 35.

The rows ask: **the new Act 5 title card** (round 62 F.9's redo: three new ideas, pick one), the production art with
game captures beside it (accept or redo), ten sounds (a or b; **a** plays in the game until you pick) and seven calls
the build made (the recommendation is pre-selected). Decided as trivia and only logged (in the parts' *Decisions*):
the large rocks at x 60 or 420 only, the Talon's `orbit` for the convoy escorts, the Mote Swarms 8 strong, a secret's
radio line without `requires`, the failed secondary's line on the first Gunship's escape, the `CONVOY` label on an
escapes objective, the turret cars counted in a chain's HP and bounty sums.

**Closed 2026-10-28**: the answers are in the Answer and Outcome columns and in the *Decisions* entry. The user picked the gold wing **c** for the Act 5 title card (not the recommendation; a later art step, ART-F), accepted the art and all seven calls, picked the sounds (b for the mine's arm, the Serpent's split, the Gunship's ring and doors; a for the rest) and asked for a redo of the Serpent's rumble (new proposals in round 64's audio step).
