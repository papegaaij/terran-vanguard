# Concept round 54 — M8 part E, Level 25 Siren Trench

## Summary

The review round of M8 part E *Level 25* ([roadmap](../../tech/roadmap/README.md#m8-parts)), built and flown:
[Level 25 "Siren Trench"](../../campaign/act-4-deep-water/level-25-siren-trench/README.md), the deep cut beyond the
abyssal plain, wholly under water: ice-rock walls closing from 400 to 260 px with 25 side caves (cave waves swim out of
them, warned 3 s ahead at their wall), the [Siren](../../enemies/naval/siren/README.md) nests on the walls' feet whose
sonar rings slow the ship and Rook (60 % for 2 s, then 3 s immune), the first
[Spiral Nautilus](../../enemies/naval/spiral-nautilus/README.md) pairs orbiting the ship and uncoiling to fire spirals,
the fork round a rock pillar, the 190 px narrows, Conamara's survey sub in a left cave (the secret) and the survey
buoy's armour patch, and the Song, where nests and Nautilus pairs come together. Answer the rows below: the production
art (accept or redo) and how dark the trench is (brighter or keep), the sounds (a, b or redo) and eight calls the
build made (accept or change; the recommendation is pre-selected). The many small rules decided on the way are in the
parts' *Decisions* logs, marked "accepted in round 54 (2026-10-23)"; they were not asked here.

`tools/art/briefing_images.py` and `tools/art/intel.py`; the sounds `tools/concept/audio/sfx_r54e.py`. The captures are

Harpoon Torpedoes L3, Depth Charges L3, two Micro-Missile Pods L3, the Pressure hull, the Sonar Pulse, Rook along);
their PNGs are frames of those runs side by side, the time in the level in each row's text. The briefing and intel
captures come from a playthrough save at Level 25 (the sensor suite at L2).

Record of the decisions: [review.html](review.html)

## In the game

The art below is already in `assets/` (the finals), and the **a** sound of every pair played in the game until the
pick (heard in the capture videos). Round 53's picks are in the game (step E5: the spawning reef's look b, the b
sounds). Level 25 plays track 11, the Act 4 B theme, over the new `europa-trench` bed (S.7's a). The frame rate of a
full medium run on the software renderer (Xvfb, llvmpipe, no capture): **190 fps** on average (Level 24: 419); the
longest frame (73–90 ms in three runs) is always the level's first frame, the first-time loads; no frame of 50 ms or
more after it.
Closed 2026-10-23: the picks are in the Decisions below; the **a** sounds already play in the game (step E5), the b picks
(S.2, S.6) replace them in step LX, the rejected files moved to `design/audio/sfx/concept/rejected/`; the backdrop and the
trench's darkness (F.1, F.2) are redone in step LX and shown again in round 55.

## Sounds

Every file is cut from Freesound's public HQ previews (a pick is rebuilt from its original afterwards with the same
settings); no source is AI-made (every description checked). The rule for all of them (round 48): real recordings, heavy
and big, muffled and pressured under water, **no bubbles, nothing that pops**. They are levelled beside the files they
stand with, so they play at the same game levels. Not listened to by Claude: checked by level, peaks, onsets,
spectrograms and the loop seams; no recommendation. The **a** files play in the game now (heard in the capture videos).
Each player is labelled with its variant letter. Reused, no row: the Barbfin's needle, the Glassfin's shatter, the wall
scrape under water, the Nautilus's spiral shots (the enemy small shot) and the survey sub (the *Meridian*'s hatch); the
caves have no sound of their own (a cave wave's warning is the edge warning).
