Concept round 54: record of the decisions

The review round of M8 part E Level 25 (roadmap), built and flown: Level 25 "Siren Trench", the deep cut beyond the abyssal plain, wholly under water: ice-rock walls closing from 400 to 260 px with 25 side caves (cave waves swim out of them, warned 3 s ahead at their wall), the Siren nests on the walls' feet whose sonar rings slow the ship and Rook (60 % for 2 s, then 3 s immune), the first Spiral Nautilus pairs orbiting the ship and uncoiling to fire spirals, the fork round a rock pillar, the 190 px narrows, Conamara's survey sub in a left cave (the secret) and the survey buoy's armour patch, and the Song, where nests and Nautilus pairs come together. Answer the rows below: the production art (accept or redo) and how dark the trench is (brighter or keep), the sounds (a, b or redo) and eight calls the build made (accept or change; the recommendation is pre-selected). The many small rules decided on the way are in the parts' Decisions logs, marked "pending the user's review in round 54"; they are not asked here.

In the game

The art below is already in assets/ (the finals), and the a sound of every pair plays in the game until you pick (heard in the capture videos). Round 53's picks are in the game (step E5: the spawning reef's look b, the b sounds). Level 25 plays track 11, the Act 4 B theme, over the new europa-trench bed (S.7's a). The frame rate of a full medium run on the software renderer (Xvfb, llvmpipe, no capture): 190 fps on average (Level 24: 419); the longest frame (73–90 ms in three runs) is always the level's first frame, the first-time loads; no frame of 50 ms or more after it.

Sounds

Every file is cut from Freesound's public HQ previews (a pick is rebuilt from its original afterwards with the same settings); no source is AI-made (every description checked). The rule for all of them (round 48): real recordings, heavy and big, muffled and pressured under water, no bubbles, nothing that pops. They are levelled beside the files they stand with, so they play at the same game levels. Not listened to by Claude: checked by level, peaks, onsets, spectrograms and the loop seams; no recommendation. The a files play in the game now (heard in the capture videos). Each player is labelled with its variant letter. Reused, no row: the Barbfin's needle, the Glassfin's shatter, the wall scrape under water, the Nautilus's spiral shots (the enemy small shot) and the survey sub (the Meridian's hatch); the caves have no sound of their own (a cave wave's warning is the edge warning).

Siren: the song and ring
