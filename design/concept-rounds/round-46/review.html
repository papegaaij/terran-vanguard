## Features

  • Three acts, 21 levels of a planned seven-act, ~50-level campaign: First Contact on the Moon, Homefront on Earth, Red Dust on Mars.
  • Bosses that fill the screen: the Brood Carrier, the Gorgon Frigate, the Harbour Kraken, the Siege Spire, the Revenant Walker and the Dust Colossus, each with its own phases and death.
  • Rook, your wingman from Act II on: he flies in the escort slot with the gun you buy him, takes hits, and talks back.
  • A Tyrian-style loadout: front and rear guns, two wing mounts, generator, shield, armour, utility modules and a special, bought and upgraded in the hangar with the credits you earn; power draw, a regenerating shield and armour that does not regenerate.
  • The pre-rendered look of the late 90s: CGI sprites and backdrops, a portrait play field with HUD side panels on a 16:9 screen.
  • Voiced radio chatter and briefings that tell the story in flight, from first contact to the twist.
  • Three difficulties, mission grades and high scores, keyboard and gamepad.
