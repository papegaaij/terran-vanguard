Concept round 44: record of the decisions

Your rule of 2026-10-20 still stands: every undecided point is a question here, with a recommendation; nothing undecided gets built. Round 43's answers were built since: the walker's second rework, Level 20's two levers and the ridge key, and M7 part H (Level 21, the Dust Colossus and the act end). The round holds, in this order:

  1. The Revenant Walker's second rework (W rows, step WR2): the capture and the numbers it was tuned to.
  2. The sounds (S rows): eighteen proposals from the Freesound database (the Colossus's six subjects, the walker's lance charge, lance shot and stomp shockwave; a, b or redo). Claude has not listened to any of them; the game plays the a variants for now (the Colossus's ridge pass plays b).
  3. Level 20's levers (L rows): the swarm's two levers, the easy and hard lunge cadence, and one limit to raise.
  4. Level 21's final art (F rows), voices (V rows), captures (K rows) and the build choices of the six steps that built it (B rows).
  5. Part H's open questions (Q rows): the wave rework the floor needs, the phase-1 calibration, the par, a safe spot, a hard HP key, the nests' zones, the Choir's take and a block of small defaults.
  6. The art audit and approvals (A rows), the new glows (G rows), Act 2 to final (X rows) and part I's questions (I rows), then Act 4 (J) and the records (N).

Part H in short. Level 21 is the end of Act 3: the CDF's rim line, the inner wall, the dune sea with the ridge that moves under the sand, the lime hollow, then the arena that crawls at 20 px/s while the Dust Colossus breaches from the four edges and, in phase 3, under the ship; its suction pulls the ship and Rook, its debris stops your shots, its spine vents shoot in phase 2. Its death is the Choir's first spoken words, the settling dust, the debrief with ACT III COMPLETE · RED DUST, the four outro pages and the release's end ("ACTS I-III COMPLETE", "ACT IV - DEEP WATER. EUROPA. COMING SOON."). It is built and flown whole by the autopilot on every difficulty (medium: the fight 101.7 s, the haul +0.7 % of the budget). Two of the level's rules fail on purpose: the floor and the gaps (Q.1), because H.1 (round 43) said to rework the waves only if they fail and only you decide the rework.
