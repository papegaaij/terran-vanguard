# Concept round 44 — M7 part H (Level 21, the Dust Colossus), the walker's second rework, the sounds, Act 2 to final, part I's questions

## Summary

Your rule of 2026-10-20 still stands: **every undecided point is a question here, with a recommendation; nothing
undecided gets built.** Round 43's answers were built since: the walker's second rework, Level 20's two levers and the
ridge key, and **M7 part H** (Level 21, the Dust Colossus and the act end). The round holds, in this order:

1. **The Revenant Walker's second rework** (`W` rows, step WR2): the capture and the numbers it was tuned to.
2. **The sounds** (`S` rows): eighteen proposals from the Freesound database (the Colossus's six subjects, the walker's
   lance charge, lance shot and stomp shockwave; *a*, *b* or *redo*). Claude has not listened to any of them; the game
   plays the *a* variants for now (the Colossus's ridge pass plays *b*).
3. **Level 20's levers** (`L` rows): the swarm's two levers, the easy and hard lunge cadence, and one limit to raise.
4. **Level 21's final art** (`F` rows), **voices** (`V` rows), **captures** (`K` rows) and the **build choices** of the
   six steps that built it (`B` rows).
5. **Part H's open questions** (`Q` rows): the wave rework the floor needs, the phase-1 calibration, the par, a safe
   spot, a hard HP key, the nests' zones, the Choir's take and a block of small defaults.
6. **The art audit and approvals** (`A` rows), **the new glows** (`G` rows), **Act 2 to final** (`X` rows) and **part
   I's questions** (`I` rows), then **Act 4** (`J`) and the **records** (`N`).

**Part H in short.** Level 21 is the end of Act 3: the CDF's rim line, the inner wall, the dune sea with the ridge
that moves under the sand, the lime hollow, then the arena that crawls at 20 px/s while the Dust Colossus breaches
from the four edges and, in phase 3, under the ship; its suction pulls the ship and Rook, its debris stops your shots,
its spine vents shoot in phase 2. Its death is the Choir's first spoken words, the settling dust, the debrief with
`ACT III COMPLETE · RED DUST`, the four outro pages and the release's end ("ACTS I-III COMPLETE", "ACT IV - DEEP WATER.
EUROPA. COMING SOON."). It is built and flown whole by the autopilot on every difficulty (medium: the fight 101.7 s,
the haul +0.7 % of the budget). **Two of the level's rules fail on purpose**: the floor and the gaps (Q.1), because H.1
(round 43) said to rework the waves only if they fail and only you decide the rework.

Open [index.html](index.html) in a browser (regenerate with

*no* (say what). Where the Answer column lists options, the first marked *(recommended)* is mine.

## The Walker's second rework

Round 43's W.a change ("The first missile hatch is destroyed before the walker has a chance of ever using it. That's
too easy. When both missile hatches are destroyed, the walker is mostly toothless.") was built in step WR2
([the walker](../../enemies/bosses/revenant-walker/README.md#duration-and-hp)): the shoulder pods are **armoured until
their hatch opens**, a back-mounted **rail lance** fires once both pods are gone (a 1 s charge line through where the
ship was, a 0.3 s heavy shot, every 4 s, every 3 s in phase 3) and the **stomp shockwave** (a 0.5 s brace with a ground
ring, then a ring out to 110 px within 90 px of the walker, so hugging it costs something). Measured with the
autopilot and the plan's Level 19 fit.
