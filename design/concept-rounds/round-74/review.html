Open. M10 part B was built on the branch m10-act-6 (local commits through 1e734936; the full ./gradlew check green on 2026-10-31 in step B5, Acts 1–5's golden flights and replays unchanged): Level 36 "Cloud Divers" plays from its briefing to the release's end, with the Act 6 intro (the title card, four voiced pages, Vorne's page 3 intercepted over track 14's motif stem), the Chimera, the Helium-3 harvesters and their secondary, the fleet's drop craft marked friendly, the foreground banks, the gusts and three lightning strikes, the soot puff under the kill flashes, track 14 "Eye of the Storm" at full length and the Jovian sky. This round asks, in this order: part B's production art to confirm beside the first Level 36 captures, the level's sounds and track 14, the act's first voices, two Level 36 questions (the harvester pair's drift, the briefing pages' length) and last the important calls the steps made, each with its recommendation. The recommendation is pre-selected on every row; leave a row alone to keep it.

The captures are the game's own (the recipe): the balance plan's Level 36 fit on medium, the ship invulnerable and left where it launches (the first gust spell pushes it to the right edge, where it stays), three short windows steered by key presses. Found while capturing: the t=120 Chimera swarm enters at its bank's edge and leaves it within a second, so no capture shows a Chimera healing under a bank; in the capture flights the first strike's column struck beside the t=136 wing, not through it (the wing swoops toward the ship; Level36Test's autopilot draws it into the column and the kill pays there).

Decided as trivia, not asked (m10b-gaps §4): the harvester and the drop craft are unit data files owned by Level 36's document; "not counted" is its own reason beside neutral (the harvester is picked and paid); its escorts orbit the living hulls' midpoint; the drop craft enter from the bottom edge with the friendly banner as their warning; the pathfinder pod is a small debris chunk that reveals its crate; a capped swarm member skips its volley; the branch's release ends after Level 36 until part H; no golden flight for Level 36 until part I; the cloud tops' tile set rolled 40 rows (B4).
