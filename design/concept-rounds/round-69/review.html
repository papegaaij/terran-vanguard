# Terran Vanguard @tag@ — Acts 1 to 5

A late-90s style vertical shoot'em up set in 2185. This release holds the first five of the campaign's seven acts: thirty-five levels on easy, medium or hard, from Earth orbit and Luna (Act 1, First Contact) over Earth's cities, oceans and arctic (Act 2, Homefront), the Mars colonies (Act 3, Red Dust) and Jupiter's moon Europa (Act 4, Deep Water) to the asteroid belt (Act 5, The Belt). Each act has its briefings, radio chatter and outro, the shop and the hangar between the levels, and a boss at its end: the Brood Carrier, the Siege Spire, the Dust Colossus, the Abyssal Maw and the Iron Sovereign. From Act 2 on Rook flies with you as your wingman.

New in this release, Act 5: seven levels in the belt, from the dock canyon of Ceres Hub as the station turns sides, through a mined shipping lane, a refugee tug to escort and the tunnels of the hollowed asteroid Kleopatra, to Ceres orbit; for the first time the enemy is human-built, with shielded ships and walkers; the Tesla Coil Pod and the Swarm Tail in the shop, the Shield Mk V and a captured Mk V "Helix" generator; and the Iron Sovereign, a battle station of rotating rings. After Level 35 the campaign stops at Act 6, Jovian Storm, which follows in a later release. A save from v0.2 carries on: Continue opens Act 5's intro.

## The game is not signed

  • Windows: SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway (only on the first start).
  • macOS: the first start is refused ("cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified"). Control-click the app, choose Open and confirm, or choose Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security. If macOS says the app is damaged, run once in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/path/to/Terran Vanguard.app".
  • Linux: if the game does not start, make it executable: chmod +x terran-vanguard.

The Apple silicon build is made the same way as the others but has not yet been started on a real Mac with an M-series chip; if it will not open even after the steps above, please report it.

## Requirements

A 64-bit system with OpenGL 3.2 or newer (any graphics chip from the last ten years); keyboard or gamepad. The game starts in borderless full screen; Alt+Enter or F11 switch to a window.
