Concept round 37: record of the decisions

The review round of M7 part A Act 3 systems (roadmap), built while the user was on vacation: the Ion Beam and its beam delivery, the Light Drone Bay and its drones, the Deflector Pod, the Rear Lance, the EMP Burst with the machine flag, the Orbital Lance, the Shield Overcharge with the cooldown rules and the HUD ring, the Evasive thrusters' dash and its pip, the third utility bay (save format 4), reference_dps 15–21 and the balance plan to Level 21. No Act 3 level exists yet, so everything is flown on Act 1–2 levels with --loadout and --special. The documents stay design: approved (round 36); every default, pick and build choice below was taken on the user's behalf and is pending the user's review. Where the round offers an a/b, the game plays a until the round closes.

Findings

seen in the captures and not fixed (Claude's reading):

  • (a) The heat tint on a Gorgon Frigate tentacle shows as a flat pure-white silhouette for over a second, more a blink than a steady tint.
  • (b) On the frigate's hull the column ends without a contact flare.
  • (c) The column runs on past the Brood Carrier in its first (high-air) phase and past the Siege Spire (Level 14, t 114) instead of stopping on them.
  • (d) The cooldown ring's white READY flash and the dash pip's white state count rendered frames (12 and 10), so at an uncapped 460 fps they last about 20–25 ms; they should count time.
  • (e) In the hangar the Shield Overcharge shows SELL 5 000 right after buying (the visit's undo refund, probably, not the 60 % sell-back) and an empty VS FITTED: line.
  • (f) The EMP Burst's FIT was refused with "NEEDS 1 MW MORE POWER" at 0 MW draw, while the Overcharge fitted in the same state (the scratch save was over its 14 MW cap).
  • (g) "1 FREE EMP BURST CHARGE" stays on the message line on every slot until the next transaction; after buying the bay the cursor lands on the locked IFF spoofer row.
Fixed in step A8

all but (c), which is left as built for your answer (the approved rule may well pass the Carrier's first phase and the Spire). Not yet seen in a new capture; each has a test (ArsenalHudTest, ActThreeHangarTest).

  • (a) The head of the Gorgon Frigate the beam burns now takes the steady heat tint every other target gets instead of the white hit flash, which the beam renewed every step.
  • (b) Where something the beam cannot damage stops it (the frigate's closed hull, a shielded part), the column now ends at its edge (not bored 4 px in) in the glance spark, the "glance at its edge" of B.1 (b); a burn's contact flare would read as damage, so it is not used there.
  • (d) The READY flash and the dash pip's white state now last 0.2 s of game time (12 simulation steps) whatever the frame rate; the dash pip's was 10 frames, it now matches the ring's 0.2 s.
  • (e) The SELL 5 000 is the designed full refund of something bought during the same visit (the sale's dialog says so; a later visit sells it at the 60 % share). The detail now says so too (BOUGHT THIS VISIT: FULL REFUND), and the VS FITTED: line is left out when nothing differs (the Overcharge shares no stat with a charge special and draws the same 0 MW).
