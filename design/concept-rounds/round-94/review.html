Concept round 94: record of the decisions

M11 part G was built (branch m11-act-7, uncommitted; the full ./gradlew check green on 2026-11-08, Acts 1–6's golden flights and replays unchanged): Level 49 "Echoes" flies through the Choir's memory of Luna, Mars and Ceres, all in a spectral tint, then the boss rush: the echoes of the Brood Carrier, the Dust Colossus and the Iron Sovereign, each told by its own warning, each followed by a refill. The three echoes were built to your boss recipe (each part its own gun and silent when destroyed, then the next stage with new tricks), and so was Vorne's Chimera, which round 93 sent back with "way too little action". This round asks, in this order: the part's art, the sounds and the music copies in place, the voices, the level and the whole rush and the whole reworked Chimera fight in play (real time, with sound), three boss calls and the important calls, each with its recommendation. The recommendation is pre-selected on every row; leave a row alone to keep it.

Decided as trivia, not asked: an echo is a variant of its original's data, so Levels 7, 21 and 35 cannot move; the tint is a look only and never touches the bullets; an echo forms in 3 s from its station, untouchable, and its shots in flight fade at its dissolve, its launched units vanishing unpaid; the second warning has no Choir line; the floor of Hellas exists only in gate 2's stretch and the station only in gate 3's; the secondary's deadline is the first warning; the databank skips the echoes; the money scale is 0.51; every new gun of the Chimera (the batteries' aimed bursts, the rack's homing missiles, the bay's fans, the sacs' spore bursts, the spine's lobs, the capsule's port bursts, the rise volley, the climb volley, the lance sweep and the twin lances) is trivia, judged in L.6.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.
