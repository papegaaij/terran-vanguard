Concept round 36: record of the decisions

The Act 3 design phase, built overnight on the user's behalf (2026-10-12, the user away and asked for it: design documents and concept proposals only, no game code). Levels 15–21 on Mars are draft level documents; the act README, the Mars setting, nine new units, two bosses, the Act 3 arsenal and the Act 3 audio briefs are drafts; every part without chosen art has an a/b proposal. Every open design question of the gap analysis (m7-gaps.md §3, M1–M19) was answered with its recommended option, and everything below is pending the user's review. Nothing here is approved until the user says so; M7's build parts A–I start from the answers.
