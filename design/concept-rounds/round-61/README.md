# Concept round 61 — M9 part A, the Act 5 systems and arsenal

## Summary

The review round of M9 part A *Act 5 systems* ([roadmap](../../tech/roadmap/README.md#m9-parts)): enemy hex shields and
`shield-breaker`, the Tesla Coil Pod and the Swarm Tail, the homing diamond, the generator drain and its HUD row, the
`laser-line`, vacuum (its bursts and wrecks) and the `IN ROCK` limit, the enemy channel on the radio and the intercepted
briefing page, the hangar's ◆ marker, `reference_dps` 29–35 and the Act 5 balance plan. No Act 5 level exists yet, so the
Tesla Coil Pod was flown on the real Level 08, and everything else on a **capture rig**: Level 08 in a scratch copy made
`vacuum` and `underground`, with shields on its Stingers, Spore Bombers and Mantises, homing diamonds from its Needlers,
laser lines from its Spine Turrets, a Vorne line, an intercepted page and three drainers latched at set seconds (the
[recipe](../../enemies/concept/prompts.md#belt-rig-capture-r61-a); nothing of it is in the repository). The art and sound
are Act 2's units standing in for the Ascendancy's.

Answer the rows below: the production art (a or b, or accept or redo), nine calls the build made (accept or change; the
recommendation is pre-selected) and the belt README to approve. Decided as trivia and only logged (the parts' *Decisions*):
the balance plan's new `unfit` step, the plan buying the generator before upgrades, and hard's Act 5 plan without the Ion
Beam.

**Closed 2026-10-28**: the answers are in the Answer and Outcome columns and in the *Decisions* entry. The user picked the Tesla arc **a**, the shield **a** (the hex lattice, not the recommendation; the swap of the game's frames to a is the later art step ART-F) and the vacuum burst **a**, accepted the rest of the art, the power row and all nine calls, and approved the belt document.

## Review

The review page is built from this file by `build/round61-tools/build_review.py` (untracked); the board is
[index.html](index.html) (`python3 tools/concept/board.py 61`). The art generators are `tools/art/act5_gear.py`,
`tools/art/explosions.py --vacuum` and `tools/art/icons.py --act5`. The captures are game recordings with the game's own
sound (Xvfb, `--bench`, `--invulnerable`); their PNGs are frames or crops of them.

## In the game

The proposal written to `assets/` plays in the game until you pick: the arc **a**, the shield **b**, the vacuum burst
**a**. The sounds are round 60's picks (S.1–S.6, S.10–S.13), still cut from the Freesound previews; the Tesla Coil Pod
zaps with the `tesla` family's final r03 a and the Swarm Tail plays the micro-missile launch a little lower.
