Concept round 61: record of the decisions

The review round of M9 part A Act 5 systems (roadmap): enemy hex shields and shield-breaker, the Tesla Coil Pod and the Swarm Tail, the homing diamond, the generator drain and its HUD row, the laser-line, vacuum (its bursts and wrecks) and the IN ROCK limit, the enemy channel on the radio and the intercepted briefing page, the hangar's ◆ marker, reference_dps 29–35 and the Act 5 balance plan. No Act 5 level exists yet, so the Tesla Coil Pod was flown on the real Level 08, and everything else on a capture rig: Level 08 in a scratch copy made vacuum and underground, with shields on its Stingers, Spore Bombers and Mantises, homing diamonds from its Needlers, laser lines from its Spine Turrets, a Vorne line, an intercepted page and three drainers latched at set seconds (the recipe; nothing of it is in the repository). The art and sound are Act 2's units standing in for the Ascendancy's.

Answer the rows below: the production art (a or b, or accept or redo), nine calls the build made (accept or change; the recommendation is pre-selected) and the belt README to approve. Decided as trivia and only logged (the parts' Decisions): the balance plan's new unfit step, the plan buying the generator before upgrades, and hard's Act 5 plan without the Ion Beam.

In the game

The proposal written to assets/ plays in the game until you pick: the arc a, the shield b, the vacuum burst a. The sounds are round 60's picks (S.1–S.6, S.10–S.13), still cut from the Freesound previews; the Tesla Coil Pod zaps with the tesla family's final r03 a and the Swarm Tail plays the micro-missile launch a little lower.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.

accept or change
The Swarm Tail's turn rates
accept or change: the turn rates are the draft's ×1.5, 360 / 360 / 360 / 390 / 390°/s, overdrive 420 (were 240 … 280).
