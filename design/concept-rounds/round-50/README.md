# Concept round 50 — M8 part A, the Act 4 systems and arsenal

## Summary

The review round of M8 part A *Act 4 systems* ([roadmap](../../tech/roadmap/README.md#m8-parts)): the under-water
mode (the drag, every shot at ×0.7 speed and damage unless `anti-sub`, the beams' reach, the torpedoes' range, the
waterline switch), the specials' limits with their words on the HUD (`UNDER ICE`, `NO WATER`), the Sonar Pulse,
the Decoy Flares, the Harpoon Torpedoes, the Depth Charges, the pressure hull, the Auto-repair nanites, the Mk IV
generator, the Bulwark heavy drone and Rook's per-level availability, `reference_dps` 22–28 and the Act 4 balance
plan. No Act 4 level exists yet, so everything was flown on Act 2–3 levels: Level 11 for the water levels' `sub`
layer (its Driftjelly fields), and Levels 18–20 with the debug option `--medium underwater@10` (dry for the first
10 s, then under water), always `--invulnerable`. Answer the rows below: the production art (accept or redo), the
sounds (a, b or redo), seven calls the build made (accept or change; the recommendation is pre-selected) and one
question ahead of Level 23. The many small rules decided on the way are in the parts' *Decisions* logs, marked
"pending the user's review in round 50"; they are not asked here.

## Review

The review page is built from this file by `build/round50-tools/build_review.py` (untracked); the board is
[index.html](index.html) (`python3 tools/concept/board.py 50 --subjects …`). The art generators are
`tools/art/act4_gear.py` and `tools/art/icons.py --act4`, the sounds `tools/concept/audio/sfx_r50a.py`. The captures
are game recordings with the game's own sound (Xvfb, `--bench`); their PNGs are single frames or a before/after pair.

## In the game

The art below is already in `assets/` (the finals) and the **a** sound of every pair plays in the game until you
pick; the Torpedo Pod keeps its chosen launch until S.6 is answered. The Sonar Pulse's ping (S.7 a) and the Decoy
Flares' launch (round 08's a) are the chosen files; nothing new is proposed for the flares.

## Sounds

settings) or, for the limit chime, synthesized. The rule for all of them (round 48, Q12 a): real recordings, heavy and
big, muffled and pressured under water, **no bubbles, nothing that pops**, no low-pass on the sfx bus. They are levelled
beside the files they stand with, so they play at the same game levels. Not listened to by Claude: checked by level,
peaks, onsets, spectrograms and loop seams; no recommendation.
