Concept round 50: record of the decisions

The review round of M8 part A Act 4 systems (roadmap): the under-water mode (the drag, every shot at ×0.7 speed and damage unless anti-sub, the beams' reach, the torpedoes' range, the waterline switch), the specials' limits with their words on the HUD (UNDER ICE, NO WATER), the Sonar Pulse, the Decoy Flares, the Harpoon Torpedoes, the Depth Charges, the pressure hull, the Auto-repair nanites, the Mk IV generator, the Bulwark heavy drone and Rook's per-level availability, reference_dps 22–28 and the Act 4 balance plan. No Act 4 level exists yet, so everything was flown on Act 2–3 levels: Level 11 for the water levels' sub layer (its Driftjelly fields), and Levels 18–20 with the debug option --medium underwater@10 (dry for the first 10 s, then under water), always --invulnerable. Answer the rows below: the production art (accept or redo), the sounds (a, b or redo), seven calls the build made (accept or change; the recommendation is pre-selected) and one question ahead of Level 23. The many small rules decided on the way are in the parts' Decisions logs, marked "pending the user's review in round 50"; they are not asked here.

In the game

The art below is already in assets/ (the finals) and the a sound of every pair plays in the game until you pick; the Torpedo Pod keeps its chosen launch until S.6 is answered. The Sonar Pulse's ping (S.7 a) and the Decoy Flares' launch (round 08's a) are the chosen files; nothing new is proposed for the flares.

Sounds

Every file is cut from Freesound's public HQ previews (a pick is rebuilt from its original afterwards with the same settings) or, for the limit chime, synthesized. The rule for all of them (round 48, Q12 a): real recordings, heavy and big, muffled and pressured under water, no bubbles, nothing that pops, no low-pass on the sfx bus. They are levelled beside the files they stand with, so they play at the same game levels. Not listened to by Claude: checked by level, peaks, onsets, spectrograms and loop seams; no recommendation.
