Concept round 88: record of the decisions

M11 part A was built (branch m11-act-7, uncommitted; the full ./gradlew check green on 2026-11-05, Acts 1–6's golden flights and replays unchanged): distorted space and its reversals, the window shield, teleport, mirror, spore clouds, the quiet's rules, the boss rush's fights with refills and checkpoints, the campaign end's screens and save, the beyond-gate limit, the Bastion Shield, the Vrell Swarm Call, the Mk VI at Level 47, the Choir Resonator and the Act 7 balance plan, all flown on stand-ins (no Act 7 level exists yet: Levels 43 and 44 are part B). This round asks, in this order: the Swarm Call drone's shell (QA-1), part A's production art to confirm with the first game captures, the Resonator in play, and the important calls the steps made, each with its recommendation. The recommendation is pre-selected on every row; leave a row alone to keep it.

Found while capturing and fixed: the Choir Resonator crashed the game when one wave front killed two units in the same step; step FIXA fixed it (with tests) and re-made the Resonator's captures in full. The captures come from a scratch capture rig: a stand-in Act 7 level over ART-B's real Level 43 and 44 tiles, stand-in Wardens and Skaters on ART-B's held frames, and Act 6 made the last act so the campaign end runs (the rig). Rig artefacts, not the game: the hard seam where Level 43's and 44's fields meet, and the Warden cycling its iris frames in the Resonator stills.

Decided as trivia, not asked: a regrowing shell counts as open; the leaving tilt is a squash to 0.86 and a 3 px lift over 0.5 s; the backdrop slides sideways only in the drift; spore clouds are 60–100 px in radius; the transit's formation point is (240, 96), eased over 2 s; a ring slides away from a blocked arrival; a mirroring unit's row has no upper cap; a landing kill counts by anyone's shot.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.
