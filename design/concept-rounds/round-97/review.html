Concept round 97: record of the decisions

Round 95 answered "ok or redo" on the Heart's and the hive's sounds with two notes: S.1 "Heartbeat is too weak, especially on my phone. Probably due to the low frequency." and S.2 "Heartbeat is again hard to hear on a phone". The cause is as you guessed: nearly all of the old beats' energy sits below what a phone speaker plays. This round has three new beats for the Heart (S.1) and three matching layers for the hive (S.2), each keeping the old deep thud and adding a body a small speaker plays. Pick a, b or c for S.1, then the one matching it for S.2. The pick goes into the game after the choice.
