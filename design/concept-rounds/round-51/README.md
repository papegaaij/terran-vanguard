# Concept round 51 — M8 part B, Level 22 and the Act 4 intro

## Summary

The review round of M8 part B *Level 22 + Act 4 intro* ([roadmap](../../tech/roadmap/README.md#m8-parts)), built and
flown: [Level 22 "Ice Shelf"](../../campaign/act-4-deep-water/level-22-ice-shelf/README.md) on Europa's ice with its
eight geyser rows, the open leads along the Skimmer runs, the four [Drill Rigs](../../enemies/ground/drill-rig/README.md)
(the secondary: easy 2, medium 3, hard all 4 cores), the core-sample cache, Conamara's ice-crawler and the run-in to
Conamara Station, Dr. Ilse Brandt on the radio; and the [Act 4](../../campaign/act-4-deep-water/README.md) intro (the
ACT IV DEEP WATER title card and four voiced pages) after Act 3's outro or from a `v0.1` save at the Level 22 hangar.
Answer the rows below: the production art (accept or redo, and one question about the backdrop's deep layer),
Dr. Brandt's casting (a or b), the sounds (a, b or redo), the two Act 4 tracks (accept or redo) and seven calls the
build made (accept or change; the recommendation is pre-selected). The many small rules decided on the way are in the
parts' *Decisions* logs, marked "pending the user's review in round 51"; they are not asked here.

## Review

The review page is built from this file by `build/round51-tools/build_review.py` (untracked); the board is
[index.html](index.html) (`python3 tools/concept/board.py 51 --subjects …`). The art generators are
`tools/art/drill_rig.py`, `tools/art/backdrop_l22.py`, `tools/art/l22_props.py`, `tools/art/portraits.py`,
`tools/art/act_stills.py`, `tools/art/briefing_images.py` and `tools/art/intel.py`; the sounds
`tools/concept/audio/sfx_r51b.py`, the music `tools/concept/audio/music_r51b.py`, the audition
`tools/concept/audio/tts_r51b.py`. The captures are game recordings with the game's own sound (Xvfb, `--bench`,
medium, `--invulnerable`, the Hammer Mortar and two Bomb Racks with four Airstrike charges); their PNGs are frames of
those runs side by side.

## In the game

and Dr. Brandt speaks with candidate **a** (b's five takes wait in her concept folder, so either pick is a file
