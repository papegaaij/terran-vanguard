Concept round 51: record of the decisions

The review round of M8 part B Level 22 + Act 4 intro (roadmap), built and flown: Level 22 "Ice Shelf" on Europa's ice with its eight geyser rows, the open leads along the Skimmer runs, the four Drill Rigs (the secondary: easy 2, medium 3, hard all 4 cores), the core-sample cache, Conamara's ice-crawler and the run-in to Conamara Station, Dr. Ilse Brandt on the radio; and the Act 4 intro (the ACT IV DEEP WATER title card and four voiced pages) after Act 3's outro or from a v0.1 save at the Level 22 hangar. Answer the rows below: the production art (accept or redo, and one question about the backdrop's deep layer), Dr. Brandt's casting (a or b), the sounds (a, b or redo), the two Act 4 tracks (accept or redo) and seven calls the build made (accept or change; the recommendation is pre-selected). The many small rules decided on the way are in the parts' Decisions logs, marked "pending the user's review in round 51"; they are not asked here.

The art below is already in assets/ (the finals), the a sound of every pair plays in the game until you pick, and Dr. Brandt speaks with candidate a (b's five takes wait in her concept folder, so either pick is a file move).
