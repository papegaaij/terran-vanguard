Concept round 53: record of the decisions

The review round of M8 part D Level 24 (roadmap), built and flown: Level 24 "Abyssal Plain", the ocean floor past Thera Deep's farms, wholly under water and darkening section by section: the plain's edge with the last grow lamps and the first Barbfins, the reef field with the four spawning reefs breeding Glassfin fry (the secondary: easy 2, medium 3, hard all 4), three Abyss Rays rising from their shadows with four Barbfins each, the line of human relay beacons pulsing every 2 s (Varga hears Helix on them), the wrecked survey sub Meridian (the secret) and the dark crossing to the trench's mouth. Answer the rows below: the production art (accept or redo; the spawning reef's look a or b), the sounds (a, b or redo; round 52's under-water explosion family asked again with its players labelled), seven calls the build made (accept or change; the recommendation is pre-selected) and one rule of Level 25 ahead of its build. The many small rules decided on the way are in the parts' Decisions logs, marked "pending the user's review in round 53"; they are not asked here.

The art below is already in assets/ (the finals; the spawning reef plays look a), the a sound of every pair plays in the game until you pick (heard in the capture videos), and round 52's under-water explosion family a still plays at every kill below the waterline.
