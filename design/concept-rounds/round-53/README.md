# Concept round 53 — M8 part D, Level 24 Abyssal Plain

## Summary

The review round of M8 part D *Level 24* ([roadmap](../../tech/roadmap/README.md#m8-parts)), built and flown:
[Level 24 "Abyssal Plain"](../../campaign/act-4-deep-water/level-24-abyssal-plain/README.md), the ocean floor past
Thera Deep's farms, wholly under water and darkening section by section: the plain's edge with the last grow lamps and
the first [Barbfins](../../enemies/naval/barbfin/README.md), the reef field with the four
[spawning reefs](../../enemies/ground/spawning-reef/README.md) breeding Glassfin fry (the secondary: easy 2, medium 3,
hard all 4), three [Abyss Rays](../../enemies/naval/abyss-ray/README.md) rising from their shadows with four Barbfins
each, the line of human relay beacons pulsing every 2 s (Varga hears Helix on them), the wrecked survey sub *Meridian*
(the secret) and the dark crossing to the trench's mouth. Answer the rows below: the production art (accept or redo; the
spawning reef's look a or b), the sounds (a, b or redo; round 52's under-water explosion family asked again with its
players labelled), seven calls the build made (accept or change; the recommendation is pre-selected) and one rule of
Level 25 ahead of its build. The many small rules decided on the way are in the parts' *Decisions* logs, marked
"accepted in round 53 (2026-10-23)"; they were not asked here.
