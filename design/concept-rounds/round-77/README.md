# Concept round 77 — M10 part E "Level 39 Io Flyby"

## Summary

**Open.** M10 part E was built on the branch `m10-act-6` (local commits through `8b3eefef`; the full `./gradlew check`
on 2026-11-01 in step E5 on a snapshot of it: 5,209 tests, every one green but five of Level 37's, in flight beside
this round — `VoiceFilesTest` ×2 for its 13 shortened lines still unvoiced (step VOX-C2) and `RadioTimelineLevel37Test` ×3
for the same lines and the old time of Rook's line (step C6b) —; Acts 1–5's golden flights and replays unchanged):
Level 39 "Io Flyby" plays from its briefing to the release's end, opening over Io's limb with Jupiter low beneath it,
with the six cracking towers of the Loki, Pele and Prometheus works as the `destroy-targets` primary (a tower leaving the
screen alive fails the mission at once), three holds over the works, 27 vents throwing burning rocks (each vent's first
tell soon after it enters, then every 5–7 s in a hold), a Strider's topple counting on a tower, the eight pump stations
of the `PUMPS n / 6` secondary with the racks' pipes going dark, the Pele gate's two Sentinels, the survey hut (the
secret) and the supply depot, track 14 with the `io-refinery` bed, and the level's voices. This round asks, in this
order: part E's production **art to confirm** beside the first Level 39 captures, the level's four new **sounds**, its
**voices**, three **Level 39 questions** (the eruptions at full scroll, the hold windows, Rook's armour) and last the
**important calls** the steps made, each with its recommendation. The recommendation is pre-selected on every row;
leave a row alone to keep it. Level 39 has no boss.

The captures are the game's own (the [recipe](../../campaign/act-6-jovian-storm/level-39-io-flyby/concept/prompts.md#level-39-capture-r77-a)):
the balance plan's Level 39 fit on medium (Level 38's guns: the Ion Beam, the Tesla Coil Pod, a Micro-missile Pod, the
Swarm Tail; Rook with his Mortar), the ship invulnerable and parked where it launches; a **topple run** (the beam alone,
steered under the Loki towers, then between the Pele Striders to cut the upper one's leg nearer Pele B) and a **failed
run** (not invulnerable, the beam alone on the left edge, Loki A left unshot). Found while capturing: in the parked run
every tower and six pumps fall (`TOWERS 6 / 6`, `PUMPS DONE`), the towers within about 4 s of their hold's start; the
hut and the depot pass unopened (the beam passes them, the pods find units); `TOWERS FAILED` shows the step a tower
leaves, the failed screen about 2.9 s later; with `--invulnerable` a passed tower shows `FAILED` but the level goes on
(the debug option keeps a lost primary from ending it).

Decided as trivia, not asked (m10e-gaps §4): the Warden Tank does not fly Level 39 (its page keeps Level 41 only); the
plume field's ripple is the authored first-tell offsets, no vent-row key; the radio events are named `first-target` and
`topple-target`; the works' vents retimed into their holds (the doc's "≈" times), each at least 100 px from every tower
and pump station.
