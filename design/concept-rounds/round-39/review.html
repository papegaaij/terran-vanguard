Concept round 39: record of the decisions

The review round of M7 part C Level 16 (roadmap), built while the user was on vacation: terrain collision (the canyon's walls and rock islands, the ship's and Rook's contact and push, shots, beams, missiles and enemy bullets against rock), the walls projected rising toward the camera from their foot (round 36 MA.1 = b), the Tendril Anchor gates (link, a beam burning the tendril) with the new links secondary and its BARRIERS tracker, the Threadcrawler as a ground chain along the walls' foot, Level 16 Valles Canyon Run's data, its Valles backdrop and props, and the part's sounds.
