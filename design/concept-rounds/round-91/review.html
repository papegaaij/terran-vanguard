Concept round 91: record of the decisions

M11 part D is built up to its captures (branch m11-act-7, uncommitted): Level 46 "Graveyard of the Choir" with the Husks, the seed casket, the warm hull with its core, the Helix wreck, Vorne's courier, the dying Leviathan, the dead-fleet backdrop, the radio voiced and the sounds in place. This round asks, in this order: the level's ambience bed (a/b), part D's art to confirm, the level in play from game captures, and the important calls, each with its recommendation. The recommendation is pre-selected on every row; leave a row alone to keep it.

The in-play rows are game captures (step CD5). The starless patch is drawn by the game's code, so it is judged in play (L.1), not as a picture.

Decided as trivia, not asked: the moved Wraith and Husk times; Vorne's courier has 20 HP on every difficulty; the Husks all spin one way; at most 6 Husks at once, by the line layout; Level 46's bounty_scale is 0.135; a hulk was dropped from the quiet's motion budget; the Husk's hull shape (of three) is picked by its serial; the starless patch is drawn for Level 46 by its level number (no data key); the hangar's long mission line drops "BEFORE"; a level name too long for the HUD is set in its small font; the seed casket has an intel portrait of its own.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.
