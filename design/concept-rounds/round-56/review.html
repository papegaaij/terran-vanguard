Concept round 56: record of the decisions

The review round of the under-water weapons rework (AW), built after your playtest of Levels 22–25 on 2026-10-23 (the Ion Beam outclassed the anti-sub weapons, the Harpoon was tuned for single targets, the Depth Charges were rolled out behind the ship and trapped a beam player). It is the rework only: part G (Level 27) has not started. You can now play Levels 22–26 with the rework; what you are asked is whether the numbers and texts are right. The build (step log in m8-gaps.md, 2026-10-23 and 2026-10-24: the playtest, AW-P, AW1–AW3, VOX-AW): the energy weapons do ×0.2 damage and reach ×0.4 under water; the Harpoon is a fast, light swarm-killer (about 3 a second, 33 damage, a 52 px splash, homing only in a ±15° cone ahead, +50 % speed); the Depth Charges are tossed about 200 px ahead over the hull and burst on their fuse or on touching a swimmer; the Barbfin is tougher; Levels 23–26's waves are retuned; the balance plan sells nothing; the warnings are voiced and shown (Okafor's intro, Varga's briefing and radio, the hangar notice). All checks are green. The recommendation is pre-selected on every row.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.
