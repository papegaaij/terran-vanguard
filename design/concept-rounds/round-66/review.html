Concept round 66: record of the decisions

The review round of M9 part F (roadmap), now built: Level 33 flies end to end on every difficulty (the autopilot wins every flight with and without Rook, LEECHES met): the Void Leech drifting in from the rear and the sides, latching on the ship's three spots and draining the generator 2 MW each (the power row's POWER DRAIN, REGEN OFF, FIRE 93%), shaken off or thrown off by the EMP; the prey wings, Talons on a refuelling hold that the leeches latch on, darken and drift down (the uneasy alliance); the Rail Bunker on the grown rock faces (never more than two lines telegraphed); drifting rocks with Asteroid Mites, the secret Claim 114, the CDF nav buoy, the Choir's line with the music ducked, track 13 with its motif muted, 18 voiced lines. The release now ends after Level 33's win ("LEVEL 34 COMING SOON").

The rows ask: part F's production art with game captures (accept or redo; pick a or b for the prey Talon's look, QF-2, and the leech's colour), seven sounds (a or b; a plays in the game until you pick), and eight calls the build made (the recommendation is pre-selected), the first two important concepts: how the rail line is dodged on a scrolling bunker (C.1) and the prey wing's timing (C.2). Decided as trivia and only logged (in the parts' Decisions): the leech's drain as two files (a whine per latch, one hum loop stepping with the count), the Rail Bunker's death sound added beside the sfx list, the bunker pairs staggered half the interval, the full mix by a stem time at t=121, the Talon's own easy pair interval (2.0 s), Claim 114's frames drawn as a blinking shack and its opening.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.
