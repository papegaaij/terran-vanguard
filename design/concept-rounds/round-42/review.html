Part F in short. The Revenant Walker strides across the casting yard (a stride arena: lanes, passes from every edge, 180° turns), fires homing missiles from two shoulder pods (a hatch tell 0.4 s before a salvo; the missiles can be shot down), burns everything behind it with a flame cone (a 0.6 s tell, the cone trigger by the ship's angle behind it) and vents its reactor after each burn (the weak spot); it topples and its wreck, the serial plates filed off, is hint 2 (the crawl, Vorne's motif alone). The Warden Tank rides the conveyor belts (the belt speed added), its turret tracks and fires a 3-shot burst, and the rear plate takes ×1.5. Furnace vents ripple across the floor (tell, burst, heavy 10 once per burst), the gantry turrets face up the line (arc: up, they fire after you pass), and the secondary is 12 of the 14 tanks.
