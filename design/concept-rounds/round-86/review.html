Concept round 86: record of the decisions

The follow-up to round 85 (Act 7 design phase, branch m11-act-7). You turned down the Choir Resonator's glassy sounds ("do not read like weapons at all", "like children's toys") and the three music re-takes (the motifs repeated, a melody cut off at 1:04). The Resonator's sounds are now a weapon's shot first, made of a cannon, a hammer on steel, a spark and a thunder crack, with the resonance at most a short low tail. The music is now written as real tunes: phrases that ask and answer and come to rest, a second theme with its own tune, sections that change only where a phrase has ended. Each music row has one take: say ok or change. Every row has the recommendation pre-selected: leave a row alone to keep it. The sounds and the music were measured, not listened to by Claude: the pre-selected answer is a default.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.
