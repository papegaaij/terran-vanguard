# Concept round 86 — Act 7: the Resonator's sounds and three tracks recomposed

## Summary

The follow-up to [round 85](../round-85/README.md) (Act 7 design phase, branch `m11-act-7`). You turned down the Choir Resonator's glassy sounds ("do not read like weapons at all", "like children's toys") and the three music re-takes (the motifs repeated, a melody cut off at 1:04). The Resonator's sounds are now a weapon's shot first, made of a cannon, a hammer on steel, a spark and a thunder crack, with the resonance at most a short low tail. The music is now written as real tunes: phrases that ask and answer and come to rest, a second theme with its own tune, sections that change only where a phrase has ended. Each music row has one take: say **ok** or **change**. Every row has the recommendation pre-selected: leave a row alone to keep it. The sounds and the music were measured, not listened to by Claude: the pre-selected answer is a default.

**Closed 2026-11-05**: the answers are in the Answer and Outcome columns and the Decisions. S.2 a, M.1 to M.3 ok ("The music is much better now."); the Resonator's fire sound (S.1) neither: round 87 re-takes it.
