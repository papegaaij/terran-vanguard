# Concept round 68 — M9 part H, Level 35 "Iron Sovereign" and Act 5's end

## Summary

**Closed 2026-10-29**: see the Answer and Outcome columns and the *Decisions* entry (all as recommended except S.2 b and S.5 b).

The review round of M9 part H ([roadmap](../../tech/roadmap/README.md#m9-parts)), now **built**: [Level
35](../../campaign/act-5-the-belt/level-35-iron-sovereign/README.md) flies end to end on every difficulty (the autopilot wins every flight with and without Rook): the
escort screen over Ceres orbit, the mass relay's flash across the ship's row at t=61, the station's outer hull with its
Rail Bunkers, SAM Nests and the Ceres lifeboat (the secret), the station rolling in to the halt, and the act boss, the
[Iron Sovereign](../../enemies/bosses/iron-sovereign/README.md): three rings of batteries turning round an armoured core (SAM Nest racks, Rail Bunker
rails, Sentinel Tower lasers, firing only inside the arc toward the ship), a ring locking when its last battery dies,
the core opening behind six shutters with its spiral, rim bays of Dart Drones and a rail line, the cook-offs and the
implosion. After the kill: Vorne's offer, the debrief with the act summary, the four voiced outro pages and the
release's end moved here (`ACTS I-V COMPLETE`, `ACT VI - JOVIAN STORM`). Track 19 at full length, 29 voiced lines.

The rows ask, in this order: **the boss fight** (the fight's length and the core's HP, C.1, flown both ways; Rook in
the arena, the EMP and the core's shield, the rail lines' origin), part H's **production art** with game clips (accept
or redo; **pick a or b** for the relay's flash, H.4), **thirteen sounds** (track 19 ok or redo, twelve a/b picks; **a**
plays in the game until you pick) and five more **calls the build made** (the recommendation is pre-selected).
Decided as trivia and only logged (in the parts' *Decisions*): step H1c's key names (`laser-line`, the spiral's
`run`, `until: {locks: n}`; the battery attacks keep their units' difficulty hooks), the kill-ratio secondary with no
tracker label (the validator forbids one), the arena warning at t=92 (as Level 28's), the specials' per-part caps kept
(QH-1: a Smart Bomb takes 4.0 % of the bar, an Orbital Lance 2.6–8.0 %), the secondary's 99 of 151 planned units at
medium, about 84 bay Darts a fight at medium, the halt place following the eased sections; and Act 5's credit curve on
hard (59 % under the expert's bound, carrying the earlier acts' deficit; medium −10.6 %; the Sovereign's paper
time-to-kill 209 s against the flown 149 s, `BalanceTest` pending), which stays with the open "hard credits" item
unless you want it looked at now.
