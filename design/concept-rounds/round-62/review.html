Concept round 62: record of the decisions

The review round of M9 part B (roadmap), now built: Level 29 flies end to end on every difficulty (the autopilot wins 9 of 9 with Hold fire kept), with the turning station (Block C's three SAM Nests dormant until t=60, its lights going red, the klaxon), the Talon wings and their squad leaders, the Asteroid Mites riding the belt rocks, the SAM Nests' homing salvos, the three wavering Ceres Wing pairs and the HOLD FIRE secondary, the Dock 9 halt held by four clamp guns (CLAMPS 4, the halt cycle), Rook's rescue and the escort swap, Harker on the enemy channel, "Hollow Rock" with its stems and Rook's sting; and the Act 5 intro (the title card, Vorne's two intercepted pages), played once on Continue from a v0.2 save. The release now ends after Level 29's win, until part H moves it to Level 35.

Answer the rows below: the production art (accept or redo, the captures beside each), the two new voices and Vorne's pitch, the music and fourteen sounds (a or b; a plays in the game until you pick), and six calls the build made (the recommendation is pre-selected). Decided as trivia and only logged (Level 29's Decisions): the rescue as a held section of its own with the 2 s ease, the Talon's 280 px swoop and its 4° aimed pair, the rocks' drift, the supply cache drawn as the default container and the armour patch dropped without a carrier, Vorne's page 3 on his neutral portrait.

Found in the captures (a code fix, not a question): Rook's radio barks run in Level 29 from the start, while he is still in the hold: kill-streak barks ("Look at you go, Lancer.", "Nice shooting!") in the halt with the Bulwark in the slot, and a bark at the last clamp gun that holds Okafor's "Bulwark, RTB" back ≈ 5 s and lets Rook's own "Miss me? Don't answer that." go stale (it never played in the capture). The level data says his barks are dropped here; the fix goes to the next game step. The rescue video shows the bug as it is.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.
