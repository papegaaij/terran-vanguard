# Concept round 72 — Act 6 "Jovian Storm": the design phase

## Summary

**Closed 2026-10-31**: the answers are in the Answer and Outcome columns and in the *Decisions* entry (everything as
recommended and drafted except **A.7 a**, **A.11 change**, **A.12 a**, **A.13 b**, **M.2 b**, **S.5 b**, **S.8 b**,
**S.11 b**, **S.13 b**, **S.16 b**, **V.3 b** and **V.5 recast**; J.12 renames Level 37 "Fall of Aurelia").

The Act 6 design phase is drafted, on the branch `m10-act-6` in a separate git worktree (nothing committed), overnight
on 2026-10-31 while the v0.3 dither fix ran in the main checkout. As for Acts 4 and 5 the drafts follow the
**recommended answers**, so every row below has its recommendation pre-selected and you only change what you want
changed. The documents are `design: draft`; nothing is built (no `data.yaml` for the act, no Java). This is round 72,
not 71: round 71 is taken by name (v0.3's `*-r71-a` captures). The refactor before M10 (RF9) lands on v0.3 first and
the branch is rebased onto it before part A.

The round asks, in your order: the twelve design questions of the gap analysis (Q.1-Q.12), the judgement calls the
drafts made (J.1-J.16, among them the aces' names and voices, the Silence's rule, the scales outside the band, the
Halo's checkpoint and the uplink), the artwork (A.1-A.15: seven units and the Plasma Arc, the two bosses, the world and
the people, a and b for each, with their worries), the music, sounds and voices (M, S and V rows, each saying where and
when it plays and what it should sound like, then each variant by how it sounds), and last the documents to approve
(D.1-D.18, one row each: approve or change). The art and sound rows were checked by measure, not by ear: the
pre-selected **a** on the sound rows is a default, not a listened recommendation. Two voice rows (V.3, V.5) offer a
re-render or recast because the takes fall outside the range.

Done before the round, decided and not asked: the Level 38-41 hand plan was redrawn for J.11's recommendation (the
Crucible from the Level 40 visit, Composite V moved to Level 41; the generator's hand plan, the act README and the
level documents follow), and the audio proposals' credits (68 rows) are in [CREDITS.md](../../../CREDITS.md).
