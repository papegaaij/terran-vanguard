Concept round 52: record of the decisions

The review round of M8 part C Level 23 (roadmap), built and flown: Level 23 "Through the Ice", the descent down the Vrell's drill shaft: the dry shaft mouth (the Harpoon, the Depth Charges and the Sonar Pulse read NO WATER), the waterline at t=25.5 where the hydro-kit switches the ship to the under-water rules (the wipe, the refit flash, the rules strip), the wet shaft with the first Glassfin schools and Eel Swarms, Thera Deep's lamp-lit farms with the five siphon pumps (the secondary: easy 3, medium 4, hard all 5), Conamara's survey probe (the secret), the Thera Deep tender's armour patch and the plain's edge; Thera Deep Control on the radio. Answer the rows below: the production art (accept or redo; the under-water kill bursts a or b), the Act 4 title card's redo with a giant geyser (a, b or keep the old), Thera Deep Control's casting (a or b), the sounds (a, b or redo, and one question about an in-game file), and seven calls the build made (accept or change; the recommendation is pre-selected). The many small rules decided on the way are in the parts' Decisions logs, marked "pending the user's review in round 52"; they are not asked here.

In the game

The art below is already in assets/ (the finals; the under-water bursts play a), the a sound of every pair plays in the game until you pick, and Thera Deep Control speaks with candidate a (b's four lines wait in his concept folder, so either pick is a file move). The Act 4 title card still shows round 51's still until the redo is picked. Level 23 plays track 10 "Thera Deep" (accepted in round 51) from the waterline, over the new bed under the ice.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.
