# Concept round 52 — M8 part C, Level 23 Through the Ice

## Summary

The review round of M8 part C *Level 23* ([roadmap](../../tech/roadmap/README.md#m8-parts)), built and flown:
[Level 23 "Through the Ice"](../../campaign/act-4-deep-water/level-23-through-the-ice/README.md), the descent down the
Vrell's drill shaft: the dry shaft mouth (the Harpoon, the Depth Charges and the Sonar Pulse read `NO WATER`), the
**waterline** at t=25.5 where the hydro-kit switches the ship to the under-water rules (the wipe, the refit flash, the
rules strip), the wet shaft with the first [Glassfin](../../enemies/naval/glassfin/README.md) schools and
[Eel Swarms](../../enemies/naval/eel-swarm/README.md), Thera Deep's lamp-lit farms with the five
[siphon pumps](../../enemies/ground/siphon-pump/README.md) (the secondary: easy 3, medium 4, hard all 5), Conamara's
survey probe (the secret), the Thera Deep tender's armour patch and the plain's edge; Thera Deep Control on the radio.
The rows below were answered on 2026-10-22 (closed, but S.10): the production art (all accepted; the under-water kill
bursts a), the Act 4 title card's redo with a giant geyser (a), Thera Deep Control's casting (b), the sounds (S.1–S.9
picked; the under-water explosion family S.10 asked again in round 53; the AI-made file replaced) and seven calls the
build made (all accepted).
The many small rules decided on the way are in the parts' *Decisions* logs, marked "pending the user's review in round
52"; they are not asked here.

the sounds `tools/concept/audio/sfx_r52c.py`, the audition `tools/concept/audio/tts_r52c.py`, the voiced lines
`tools/art/voice.py`. The captures are game recordings with the game's own sound (Xvfb, `--bench`, medium,
`--invulnerable`, the balance plan's Level 23 fit: Harpoon Torpedoes L3, Depth Charges L3, two Micro-Missile Pods, the
Sonar Pulse, Rook along); their PNGs are frames of that run side by side, the time in the level in each row's text.

levels. Not listened to by Claude: checked by level, peaks, onsets, spectrograms and loop seams; no recommendation. The
**a** files play in the game now (heard in the capture videos).
