# Concept round 48 — the Act 4 "Deep Water" design phase

## Summary

The Act 4 design phase, **drafted overnight on your behalf**, on branch `m8-act-4` (a separate worktree from Act 3's
`m7-act-3`; nothing of it is in the game yet). Everything below is a draft for you to judge: the act's story and
levels 22–28, the new units, the Abyssal Maw, the arsenal, the under-water rules, plus the art and music proposals.
Your rule of 2026-10-20 holds: **only artwork, sounds, boss fights and important game concepts are asked**; no build
yes/no lists. Each question has its **recommendation marked** (the drafts were written from it: change the answer and
I redraft that part). Rows run in this order: the twelve questions, the judgement calls the drafting steps flagged, the
art (units, boss and cruiser, Europa, gear), the music, the sounds not made yet, and the documents to approve.

**Sounds:** the Freesound daily quota (500 downloads) ran out again (HTTP 429), so **no sound effect was made**;
only four pieces of music (synthesized, my own score; I cannot listen, they are checked by loudness and the loop
seam). The sound-effect briefs and their candidate recordings are listed so the next round can start at once.

## Review

The review page is built from this file by `build/round48-tools/build_review.py` (untracked); the proposals are in the
parts' `concept/` directories. The board `index.html` shows every image side by side.
