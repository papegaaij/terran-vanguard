Concept round 48: record of the decisions

The Act 4 design phase, drafted overnight on your behalf, on branch m8-act-4 (a separate worktree from Act 3's m7-act-3; nothing of it is in the game yet). Everything below is a draft for you to judge: the act's story and levels 22–28, the new units, the Abyssal Maw, the arsenal, the under-water rules, plus the art and music proposals. Your rule of 2026-10-20 holds: only artwork, sounds, boss fights and important game concepts are asked; no build yes/no lists. Each question has its recommendation marked (the drafts were written from it: change the answer and I redraft that part). Rows run in this order: the twelve questions, the judgement calls the drafting steps flagged, the art (units, boss and cruiser, Europa, gear), the music, the sounds not made yet, and the documents to approve.

Sounds: the Freesound daily quota (500 downloads) ran out again (HTTP 429), so no sound effect was made; only four pieces of music (synthesized, my own score; I cannot listen, they are checked by loudness and the loop seam). The sound-effect briefs and their candidate recordings are listed so the next round can start at once.

A read-only record: every row as the review page showed it, with the answer and the outcome from the round README (the record of truth). Media is linked from the design tree, not copied.

The twelve open questions of the Act 4 gap analysis. Each lists its options, its costs and what I already drafted from the recommendation.

Q1

How hard the water bites

Concept
How hard the water bites
Concept Drafted table: every non-anti-sub weapon halved, bullets x0.7, drag, shield regeneration -25 %, beam range halved. The typical loadout (Ion Beam, missiles, Tail Gun) has no anti-sub item, so at Level 23 it loses more than half its damage and needs about 5,300 credits of new weapons against a 3,000 visit. (a) As drafted. (b) Softened: keep the feel (drag, bullets x0.7, torpedo range +50 %, beam reach x0.75) and the pressure (non-anti-sub x0.7), drop the top-speed and shield-penalties, the hydro-kit stays free and automatic, Depth Charges become a rear launcher of sinking charges. (c) No penalty, anti-sub x1.5. Costs: (a) a hard economy and power wall at the act start; (b) a real reason to buy torpedoes without scrapping the old loadout; (c) makes anti-sub optional. Drafted from (b): the weapons, Harpoon Torpedoes, Depth Charges and the systems README; the levels' enemy counts assume it. Recommendation: b

Concept
Generator Mk IV: 20 MW
Concept The roadmap drew the Mk IV Tokamak at 18 MW for 9,000 credits.
