Concept round 64: record of the decisions

The review round of M9 part D (roadmap), now built: Level 31 flies end to end on every difficulty (the autopilot keeps every section home on easy and medium and at least two on hard, with Rook, without him and with the Bulwark), the act's escort: the mining tug Bellwether holds its station on the ground layer, 200×300 px, with three sections (bow, cargo, engines) that only the hooked attacks hurt: Hornet salvos of homing diamonds on the bow, Dart streams from behind on the engines, Shard Drone links dragged across the hull; the Buzzsaw Drones chase the ship and ricochet off the big rocks; the HUD's TUG n / 3 with its three bars and the CLEAN HULL tag, the radio's {section}, the Bellwether's twelve lines, Dacosta's claim (the secret, a tow), track 13 "Gilded Cage" with its motif stem, the tug's engine bed. The release now ends after Level 31's win ("LEVEL 32 COMING SOON") until part H moves it to Level 35.

The rows ask: round 61's and round 63's art picks in production (the hex shield lattice, the gold-wing title card), part D's production art with game captures (accept or redo; pick a or b for the tug's proportions and the Buzzsaw's gold), the Bellwether's casting (a or b), track 13 at full length, eleven sounds (a or b; a plays in the game until you pick, the serpent rumble's redo included) and eight calls the build made (the recommendation is pre-selected), the first an important concept: a tow's secret against the beam. Decided as trivia and only logged (in the parts' Decisions): the Hornet's salvo interval: 4.4 (a burst's interval counts from its last shot), the hatch glow as the gun's tell, the Mite waves of 5 as two rock waves at the same t, the two large rocks at t=121/138 entering 2.5 s earlier, the armour patch at the cargo's place whatever is left, the ore haulers falling back at the backdrop's 120 px/s cap, the bounty scale 0.36, the Hornet's pod hatch as round 62's nest hatch pitched up, the Buzzsaw's and Shard Drone's deaths as their small rungs, Dacosta's name as written.
