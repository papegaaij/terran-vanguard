## swarm-tail

wing pods' cyan, so a player tells his rear swarm from the wing pods' at a glance; the plume stays pale (no orange or
yellow: those hues are the enemy bullets'). Launched backwards at 260 px/s, accelerating, curving onto targets
behind and beside the ship.
