# Player weapons — concept prompts

## projectiles-r08-a

Round 08. Mockup generated by `tools/concept/vfx_r08.py projectiles`
(`projectiles-r08-a.png` sheet and `projectiles-r08-a.gif` loop of every family firing in
sequence from the Stormhawk at L3, Ion Beam at L5).

Families: pulse (Pulse Cannon), vulcan (Scatter Vulcan), ballistic (Autocannon Pod, Swivel
Gun), laser (Lance Laser), beam (Ion Beam), missile (Hornet Launcher), micro-missile
(Micro-missile Pod), mortar (Hammer Mortar, ground only), bomb (Bomb Rack, ground only),
torpedo (Torpedo Pod), mine (Proximity Mines), rear (Tail Gun, Fan Blaster), side (Side
Splitter). Each with muzzle flash (3 frames), impact (4 frames) and L1/L3/L5 patterns.

**Prompt:** sprite sheet of player projectiles for a late-1990s pre-rendered CGI vertical
shoot'em up, top-down view, 960x540 game scale, transparent background: glowing cyan-and-white
energy bolts with soft bloom, thin white tracer slugs with pale blue tails, long piercing cyan
laser lances, a continuous rippling ion beam, small white-and-blue guided missiles with blue-
white exhaust plumes and grey smoke trails, finned grey bombs, plasma mortar shells with a
blue glowing band, slim grey torpedoes with bubble trails, squat proximity mines with a
blinking blue sensor light; star-shaped muzzle flashes, white spark impacts and small
fireballs; chrome-and-neon palette (blue, cyan, white only for player shots), crisp
silhouettes, Raptor / Tyrian 2000 era render look.

**Negative prompt:** magenta, pink, yellow or orange shots (reserved for enemy bullets), flat
vector art, cartoon outlines, photographic realism, motion blur smears, side view,
perspective camera, text, watermark.

## beam-impact-r09-a

Round 09. Mockup generated by [tools/concept/vfx_r09.py](../../../../tools/concept/vfx_r09.py) `python3 tools/concept/vfx_r09.py beam`
(`beam-impact-r09-a.png` sheet and `beam-impact-r09-a.gif` loop). Answers the round-08 feedback
that the Ion Beam stopped abruptly at the sprite edge: the beam now bores 4 px into the target's
silhouette, narrowing and turning white-hot, and ends in a contact flare (6-frame loop, three
sizes for small / medium / large targets) with sparks thrown back towards the ship, a scorch and
hot spot on the target that grow while the beam stays on, and a 1-2 px heat shimmer. With no
target the beam runs off the top edge without a cap. Shown on a Needler, a Gilded Gunship and
the Brood Carrier's bow, and in a GIF where the beam sweeps across targets and through the gaps.

**Prompt:** close-up sprite effect for a late-1990s pre-rendered CGI vertical shoot'em up,
top-down view, transparent background: a continuous cyan-and-white ion beam striking the
underside of a dark armoured gunship, the beam narrowing to a white-hot point that sinks into
the hull, a bright white contact flare with a horizontal anamorphic streak and a cyan bloom, a
thin crescent of energy splashing along the hull plates, short white and pale-cyan sparks
spraying back and sideways, a darkened scorch ring with a glowing cyan rim on the metal, faint
heat shimmer; additive glow, crisp pixel edges, Raptor / Tyrian 2000 era render look.

**Negative prompt:** orange or red heat glow (reserved for enemy bullets), beam cut off flat at
the sprite edge, smoke clouds hiding the target, lens dirt, photographic realism, side view,
text, watermark.

## arsenal-capture-r37-a

Concept round 37 (M7 part A close-out, step A7), a capture of the game, not generated art (`.png`
labelled sheet, `.mp4` H.264/AAC with the game's sound, 53 s, the full 960×540 frame with a caption
strip). Method (the four r37 captures share it): the install script of `:desktop:installDist` (the
2026-10-13 snapshot) on a private Xvfb display, settings: a 960×540 window at 0,0,
`controls.auto-fire=true`, `audio.master=0.5`; every run with `--bench` and a scratch `--settings`
file; recorded with `ffmpeg -f x11grab -framerate 30`; sound: OpenAL Soft's `wave` driver
(`ALSOFT_CONF` with `drivers = wave`) writing the game's mix to a file, nothing played aloud,
aligned by the file's end time less 0.25 s (the game's shutdown after its last sample; checked on
the EMP ring: its sound within 0.07 s of the ring's first frame). The ship was moved by XTest key
events on that display (left/right holds of 0.35 s every 1.2 s, V = dash, X = special). Level t =
video time − 7.1 to 7.9 s (each run's first level frame). Every run: `--start level --loadout
front=ion-beam:2,left=light-drone-bay,right=deflector-pod,utility=evasive-thrusters`. Runs used here:

- **Level 05, medium** (`--level 5 --difficulty medium --invulnerable --special emp-burst:3
  --bench 192`), no keys until t 178: the ship stays on the centre line, under the Gorgon Frigate
  (t 150). The deflector catches at t 51 come from this run (`--invulnerable` keeps the Deflector
  Pods catching; it stops the drones being hit, so the drone run had none).
- **Level 05, hard** (`--level 5 --difficulty hard --special smart-bomb:3 --bench 75`, sways,
  dashes), **scratch data:** a copy of [light-drone-bay/data.yaml](../light-drone-bay/data.yaml)
  with level 1 `drone_hits: 2` (the real file: 8) placed first on the run's classpath only (a copy
  of the start script with `CLASSPATH=<scratch>:$APP_HOME/lib/…`; the real file is unchanged): in
  two earlier 60 s runs on hard with the real data the drone took three or four hits and never
  broke. A Skitter rammed it at t 27.6.
- **Level 07, medium** (`--level 7 --difficulty medium --invulnerable --special orbital-lance:2`):
  the beam under the Brood Carrier (t 107–113). **Level 14, medium** (`--level 14 --escort none
  --invulnerable --special smart-bomb:3`): the beam under the Siege Spire (t 114, sheet only).

The sheet: the Ion Beam on the Gorgon Frigate (stopping at a tentacle with the contact flare, at the
hull, on the opened core), the Brood Carrier and the Siege Spire with the column running through
them; the drone's orbit, the ram, the break spark, the rebuild ring and fade-in (zoom 2.4×), the
HUD's hits strip under the `L LIGHT DRONE BAY` row (full, refilling dim, full again); the
Deflector Pod's field arc catching two bullets (white flash) at t 51.2–51.6. The mp4: Level 05 t
150–178 (the frigate fight to its death), Level 07 t 107–113, Level 05 hard t 23–36 (drone break and
rebuild), Level 05 t 48.5–54 (the catches).

Findings (pending the user's review): the beam's tint on the tentacle reads as a flat pure-white
silhouette over a second rather than a tint; on the frigate's hull the column just ends, with no
contact flare; the column runs on past the Brood Carrier in phase 1 (on `high-air`, which the Ion
Beam reaches) and past the Siege Spire at t 114: to check whether these parts are meant to stop it.
