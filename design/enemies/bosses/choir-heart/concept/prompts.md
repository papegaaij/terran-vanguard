## choir-heart-r84-a

"The heart". **Prompt:** a late-90s pre-rendered CGI final-boss sprite seen from straight above, filling the top of a
portrait play field: a vast luminous organ, a lobed heart-shaped mass of near-black violet tissue ribbed top to bottom
with soft red-violet light that swells with a slow pulse, vessels over its shoulders, six translucent pale violet
petal membranes on its lower rim (closed buds; opened, each shows a singer organ glowing violet, crystal, lime, teal,
violet-white or pink), a sealed seam down its middle that splits open on a lime-white veined core; lime knots of
nerve in the tissue walls; hung in a dark chamber of converging tissue ribs lit only by its glow.
**Negative prompt:** photorealism, gore, flat vector, cartoon, text, watermark, hard flashes, clipped glow.

## choir-heart-r84-b

"The crown". As a, but a vast domed organ whose visible lower face is banded with concentric glowing rings and fine
spokes (the Choir Herald's ring markings), a sealed boss over the core that splits, the six membranes as mouths set
along the dome's rim.
