## choir-heart-r84-a

"The heart". **Prompt:** a late-90s pre-rendered CGI final-boss sprite seen from straight above, filling the top of a
portrait play field: a vast luminous organ, a lobed heart-shaped mass of near-black violet tissue ribbed top to bottom
with soft red-violet light that swells with a slow pulse, vessels over its shoulders, six translucent pale violet
petal membranes on its lower rim (closed buds; opened, each shows a singer organ glowing violet, crystal, lime, teal,
violet-white or pink), a sealed seam down its middle that splits open on a lime-white veined core; lime knots of
nerve in the tissue walls; hung in a dark chamber of converging tissue ribs lit only by its glow.
**Negative prompt:** photorealism, gore, flat vector, cartoon, text, watermark, hard flashes, clipped glow.

## choir-heart-r84-b

"The crown". As a, but a vast domed organ whose visible lower face is banded with concentric glowing rings and fine
spokes (the Choir Herald's ring markings), a sealed boss over the core that splits, the six membranes as mouths set
along the dome's rim.

## choir-heart-final-r95-a

**The Choir Heart, production** (round 95, M11 part H, step ART-H, 2026-11-07; pending the user's review). Files:
`choir-heart-final-r95-a.png` (sheet) and `.gif` (a scripted pass at 1×). Generator: `python3 tools/art/choir_heart.py`
(signed-distance models on the frozen `tools/concept/render/` package; the review's chamber is round 84's stand-in,
`tools/concept/act7_r84.py`'s `chamber_bg`, until step ART-H2's hive-core kit).

- **Prompt:** as [choir-heart-r84-b](#choir-heart-r84-b), production quality, straight top-down, 440×300 px in play
  with its upper third beyond the top edge: a vast dome of near-black violet tissue banded with five glowing ring
  ridges and fine curved spokes (the Choir Herald's ring markings), clusters of olive sacs with lime veins on its
  shoulders (the Spore Bomber's), a sealed boss over the core marked with an eight-armed whorl (the Whirl Seeds'
  spiral); the six membranes on its rim as pale violet mouths whose six petals iris open on a singer organ in its
  unit's colour (a violet ring of orbs, crystal thorns, a lime sac, a teal seed with magenta blades, a violet-white
  whorl, a pink cluster of motes); its red-violet light swelling with a slow pulse (smooth, below a bullet's white
  core); soft key light from the top left. The closing: the dome flattening and clenching, its rings standing out.
  The opening: the dome splitting along the whorl's eight spokes from the core and the flaps peeling back on a
  lime-white veined core, light leaking from the splits. Phase 2: ribbed tissue walls with a lit violet-white inner
  edge, lime knots of nerve on their faces, fleshy pits whose lips part. K: the dead Heart greying and collapsing
  inward, lime-white bursts running out from the core.
- **Negative prompt:** photorealism, gore, flat vector, cartoon, text, watermark, hard flashes, clipped glow, ordered
  dither, straight seams or ruler-straight lines.
