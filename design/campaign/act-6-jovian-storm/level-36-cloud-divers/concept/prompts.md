# Level 36 – Cloud Divers: concept prompts and briefs

## Round 74 — production: the Jovian kit, the harvester, the drop craft and the pod (M10 part B)

Generators: `tools/art/backdrop_l36.py` (`backdrop-final-r74-a.png/.gif`, `backdrop-proposal.yaml`) and
`tools/art/jovian_units.py harvester fleet pod` (`harvester-final-r74-a`, `fleet-stormhawk-final-r74-a`,
`pathfinder-pod-final-r74-a`, `.png/.gif`). Generated by code, not by an image model: the briefs below are what the
generators were built to show (artist briefs for a polished repaint). Every band, haze, cloud, plume and glow is a
smooth gradient (far more than 32 steps), no ordered dither, drawn once (the 2026-10-30 dither audit).

- **backdrop** — The dive over Jupiter's upper atmosphere, no ground, in round 72's L36 grade (Q10 a): high sunlit
  bands in cream, ochre and rust under a pale haze, the sun from the top left. The deep layer is one continuous field
  down the level: the first screen shows Jupiter's limb bulging toward the viewer with black space and a thin blue-white
  rim below it, then the bands far below under a heavy cool haze with the cloud decks' small heads; cream and ochre
  storm bands with festoons; a broad rust band between cream edges; the band browning to grey toward the storm, then
  paler and warmer at the end. Deep pieces: the CDF carrier group far below (a long white transport hull with a blue
  spine stripe and two escorts, hazed toward the bands, nose up the screen) with its running lights (white strobes,
  red and green nav lights, blue engines) blinking; the storm wall of Level 38 on the right, a towering brown-grey
  cloud mass with lightning flickering inside it (soft blue-white flashes behind the cloud, a short bolt). Low-air:
  cream cloud tops rushing past in the dive, thin cream wisps along the wind after. High-air: faint white ice streaks in
  the dive; the foreground banks (A.12 a, the cream veil): lumpy cumulus masses in the band's own lit cream, lit tops
  and a slightly darker underside, at most 65 % opaque, soft frayed edges. The user, round 72: clouds at different
  altitudes do not have different colours (the deep heads, the tops, the wisps and the banks share one cream).
- **harvester** — An uncrewed Ascendancy helium-3 scoop (QB-1 a, no round-72 proposal): a long gunmetal hull
  (5a606c) lying across the screen, nose to the side it flies, a gold nose cap and three gold trim bands, a dark dorsal
  spine, a wide intake scoop slung under its front half with its dark mouth forward, two engine nacelles at the stern
  with orange nozzles, a gold tail fin, red running lights on the scoop's tips, the stern and the fin; 96×56, one
  render per direction (lit from the top left, never mirrored). Its pale cream gas plume trails from the scoop behind
  the hull, widening and thinning out over about 115 px, its turbulence flowing back (4 frames). Destroyed: the hull
  breaks along a jagged cut, the halves part, tip and sink into the band (shrinking, fading toward the band's colour),
  grey smoke rising from the cut, the plume flaring once along its length with fire at the cut.
- **fleet-stormhawk** — The CDF drop craft: the Stormhawk airframe (the player's model) in the fleet's colours: CDF
  grey hull, the CDF blue band and fins, white wing bars, blue engines; 40×40 at 16 headings through the Stormhawk's
  camera; a blue-white twin exhaust streak while it dives up the screen at 400 px/s.
- **pathfinder-pod** — The scouts' cargo pod: a short CDF-grey capsule with a blue band round its middle, orange hazard
  stripes at both ends, two short fins and a white beacon on top, 32×32 at 16 turns of a slow tumble; shot open in air:
  grey shell chips flung out in a ring and a puff of smoke rising and thinning, a brief orange flash at its core.
