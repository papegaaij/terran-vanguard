### backdrop-final-r80-a

- **Brief:** Callisto orbit from straight above, in vacuum, the Act 6 finale: black space with sparse, mostly faint
  stars and Ganymede as a point. Along the right edge the whole level, Jupiter's limb far off: a thin cream-orange
  crescent of bands (cream, ochre, rust) with a fine bright line on the limb, its brown-black night side beyond. Below,
  the whole level, **Callisto's curve**: a gently curved limb across the lower third, the night side under it in dark
  blue-grey ice saturated with craters, lit only by Jupiter's warm shine from the right, a thin cool rim on the limb;
  on the ice the black citadel shows only by its lights: gold window strips, a ring of lamps round the keep, the outer
  line's lamps on an arc, white lamps along two causeways, a few far outposts. The CDF carrier group far off rises
  over the night side at the start (tiny dark hulls nose up, white strobes, blue engines), the home fleet's gold lamps
  and amber drives far ahead at the top. Then two **burning hulks** of the home fleet fall toward Callisto: a Regent
  cruiser's bow half and its stern half, gloss black and gold under soot, broken along a jagged line, fire venting
  orange-white at the breaks, a trail of sparks above each, passing behind Callisto's limb. High ahead the
  **Ascendant**: a gold speck stern-on with a white-gold drive point and its plume, the sun glinting off its gold once
  in two seconds; as the wing closes its drive glow grows down from the top edge, then swings away out of the top as
  it comes about. Behind the fight nothing moves. After the escape, the lifeboat's last flash at the top edge (a
  white-teal four-pointed star, then a dwindling drive point) and the CDF fleet's lights closing in from below.
- **Readability:** the Ascendancy's gold units and amber bullets over black space (mean luminance about 11, the
  brightest 200×96 window about 28: the curve's rim and the citadel's lamps); the gold lamps on the night side kept
  small and dim; no red anywhere in the backdrop (the spinal lance's line and the batteries' rims are red); the bow's
  gold over black in the arena, Jupiter's crescent only beside its right shoulder.
