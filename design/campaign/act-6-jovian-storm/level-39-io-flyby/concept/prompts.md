# Level 39 – Io Flyby: concept prompts and briefs

## Round 77 — production: Io, the refinery kit and the refinery units (M10 part E)

Generators: `tools/art/backdrop_l39.py` (`backdrop-final-r77-a.png/.gif`, `backdrop-proposal.yaml`) and
`tools/art/refinery_units.py tower pump props` (`refinery-final-r77-a.png/.gif`). Generated by code, not by an image
model: the briefs below are what the generators were built to show (artist briefs for a polished repaint). Every
haze, plume, shadow and glow is a smooth gradient (far more than 32 steps), no ordered dither, drawn once (the
2026-10-30 dither audit); every glow additive.

### backdrop-final-r77-a

- **Brief:** Io's surface from straight above in round 72's L39 grade: desaturated sulphur yellow and ochre plains with
  low relief lit by a harsh small Sun from the top left, black lava flows as blots and lobes, soft white frost
  patches; no weather, no rising smoke. The level opens over Io's limb: black space with a few stars and two moons as
  points, Jupiter's banded disc (cream, ochre, rust, the Red Spot near its limb) huge and low at the bottom, Io's
  gently curved edge coming down from the top with a dark grazing rim, and Tvashtar's umbrella plume hanging off the
  limb against space, pale and sunlit on its left, swelling and settling slowly. Then the plains; per section a
  dressing on one periodic terrain: a graded yard road and a pipeline rack (two steel pipes on cross-members, gold
  collars) at Loki; a lava channel (a deep red-orange core under dark crust plates, thin bright cracks, black crust
  banks) with the rack beside it on the pipeline run; Pele's red-orange fallout and a road at Pele; pale fallout and
  dark vent crusts with frost rims on the plume field; frost fields at Prometheus. The three works from one kit: a
  concrete yard slab with 40 px plates and rust or fallout or frost stains, octagonal tower pads, storage spheres with
  a gold band, a control shed with a red lamp, lamp posts, the rack through the yard; each tower's pipe run to the
  rack with a faint orange hot line, each pump station's rack segment likewise (the lines go dark when it dies). The
  Pele gate: a charcoal wall with gold coping across the field, a gateway over the road, two square blockhouses for
  the Sentinels with red lamps. The supply depot: a small ribbed-roof hangar, crates on its apron, its door facing
  down the screen. The survey hut: a pale grey Coalition prefab with a CDF-blue stripe half buried in drifted sulphur
  on a black lava island in a glowing pool, its door dark. Cold vents: a black mouth in a ragged pale crust rim with
  rust stains. Pele's plume haze: thin pale sulphur veils drifting over at most 16 %.
- **Readability:** the gold units and the amber bullets over the brightest sulphur: the plains stay desaturated and a
  step darker than the units' gold (round 72's mean 115), the lava channel a deep red-orange under crust.

### refinery-final-r77-a

- **cracking tower** (96×96, hitbox 72×72) — a tall refinery cracking column seen from straight above through a mild
  perspective: an octagonal concrete footing, a tapered dark gunmetal column whose side shows as a ring band round the
  top, three desaturated gold collars, the gold flare cap at the middle with four red lamps and a burning flame (a
  white-hot core, an orange corona with flickering tongues, additive), three pipe stubs leaving the base east, west
  and south; its long soft shadow on the ground to the bottom right.
- **cracking tower fall** (160×160, 12 frames) — Io's near-vacuum: a short white flash; the column snaps above its
  second collar, the top with the flare cap tumbles off to the bottom right and lands beside the stump; a ring of
  burning chunks flung out along the ground (they do not rise), the fires round the broken lip dying down; no smoke.
- **cracking tower wreck** (96×96) — the scorched hollow stump on its footing, the top with its dead cap lying beside
  it; no glow.
- **pump station** (40×40, hitbox 32×32) — a black pump housing straddling the rack's pipes: a motor drum with a gold
  band, cooling fins, gold flanges on the pipe, a red lamp. Its wreck: the housing burst open and scorched, the lamp
  dead.
- **depot latch** (24×24, 2 frames) — an orange clamp with an amber lamp on a charcoal plate; then shot open, the jaws
  bent outward, the lamp dead.
- **survey hut lock** (24×24, 2 frames) — a dark, unlit lock box on a plate (the hut has no lights); then blown out, a
  ragged hole.
