# Level 37 – Fall of Aurelia: concept prompts and briefs

## Round 75 — production: the Aurelia kit, the turn and the falling city (M10 part C)

Generator: `tools/art/backdrop_l37.py` (`backdrop-final-r75-a.png/.gif`, `growth-final-r75-a.gif`,
`backdrop-proposal.yaml`; the shared Jovian pieces in `tools/art/jovkit.py`). Generated by code, not by an image model:
the briefs below are what the generator was built to show (artist briefs for a polished repaint). Every haze, smoke,
fire and glow is a smooth gradient (far more than 32 steps), no ordered dither, drawn once (the 2026-10-30 dither
audit); every glow is additive.

- **backdrop** — Aurelia, the Ascendancy's gilded capital, seen from straight above, hanging over Jupiter's warm cream
  cloud deck (round 72's L37 grade, Q10 a: gold and white over cream, the sun from the top left). Octagonal decks of
  pale cream plating in Art Deco rings and sectors with gilded rims, black edges and red rim lights; gold-glass domes
  with ribs and a cream collar, little cream domes in fours; promenades of square tiles; walkways with gold rails and
  warm lamps linking everything over the cloud; the decks a step paler and less saturated than the Ascendancy units'
  gold (gold on gold must read). Landmarks by the level's times: the outer rim's decks, a deck edge for the first
  nest, the rim's air-defence platform with two hexagonal SAM sockets, two shuttle pad decks (dark slate pads with a
  white ring, an amber inner ring and a cross, green-white lights) at x 190 and 290, a great dome deck, a pad terrace
  between two deck edges with a striped awning over the CDF locker on the left, the Spire Plaza (an elongated octagon
  paved in concentric rings with gold inlay circles, reflecting pools, pavilions, a terrace in front) round the great
  spire (stepped cream and gold octagonal tiers rising to a needle with a red light, a long shadow), three SAM sockets
  on its far side; teal Vrell veins creeping into the dome seams from section 2's end. After the turn every deck burns:
  teal tissue patches with bright veins and tendrils, buckled plates, soot, broken dark domes, fires glowing orange.
  The deep layer: cream cloud heads (the one cream) over warm ochre gaps far below, opening on the bands at the end;
  the city's far side hazed far below; dark smoke streaks drifting over after the turn. Low-air: tethered lift balloons
  (cream envelopes with gold bands, a red light, a cable), anti-grav rings (a gold torus with a pale blue glow),
  burning Ascendancy hulks tumbling past with smoke trails, deck plates and a cable coil after the fall. Deep: the war
  far below (tiny gold craft weaving through teal swarm clumps, tracers, small flashes).
- **growth** — The Vrell turn (t=100): over the plaza on the screen, teal tendrils race along the paving's seams from
  the dome rims inward over 4 s, tissue swelling over the plates, plates buckling, fires breaking out with soot round
  them; one image of the post-turn look, a grey reveal map (each pixel's time) and its additive glow.
- **falling city** — A.13 b with round 73 R.2's storyboard: the main deck (two great dome decks on a central spine,
  burning and overgrown) breaks along a jagged fault between the domes - sharp zigzags, never a straight line - into
  two layers; on each torn edge the plate is charred dark, flaps of plating bend outward into the gap, the rim glows
  orange-red and fires vent from it, smoke clings to both edges; shards of gold and cream plating, a few still glowing,
  tumble in the widening gap. The halves slow, darken toward a smoky brown-black and shrink toward the field's centre,
  each at its own rate, turning a little opposite ways; the lift balloons over the far edge tear along a ragged rip and
  collapse (fabric, no pop); tall stepped gold spires topple as the wing flies down beside them; at the end the city
  is a dark burning shape sinking into the bands with smoke rising off it.

## fall-final-r75-b

Generator: `tools/art/backdrop_l37.py` (round 75 F.6 redo, step ART-C3: `fall-final-r75-b.png/.gif`, the new
`fall-veil-west/-east` pieces and the halves' new curve in `backdrop-proposal.yaml`; `--review-a` rebuilds round 75 a's
sheets). Generated by code; every cloud and fade smooth (no ordered dither).

- **falling city (redo)** — As the round's falling city, but the city never leaves through an edge and never hands
  over to a smaller image: the two halves alone sink from the break to the end, slowing, shrinking toward a point a
  little above the field's centre, turning opposite ways and darkening toward smoky brown-black while their gap stays
  open; from about 12 s after the break every pixel of both (their torn rims included) stays on the screen, and late
  they sink slowly. They fade slowly the whole way - barely at first, about two thirds at 18 s, gone at 34 s - so the
  cloud deck shows through them more and more.
- **cloud tops** (`fall-veil-west/-east`, 288×196) — Patches of the cloud deck's own heads, as far below as the deck:
  cumulus heads in the one cream, lit from the top left, a fifth toward the pale haze and under the smoke haze of the
  burning sky, denser toward the city and breaking into separate heads at the outer side; each slides in from its side
  of the screen as the halves fade and closes over them (fully opaque in its middle by 24-26 s), then fades into the
  deck after the city is gone, before the level ends.
