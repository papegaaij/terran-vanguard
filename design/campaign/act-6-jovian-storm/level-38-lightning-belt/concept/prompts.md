# Level 38 – Lightning Belt: concept prompts and briefs

## Round 76 — production: the storm-layer kit, the cargo pod and the canister (M10 part D)

Generators: `tools/art/backdrop_l38.py` (`backdrop-final-r76-a.png/.gif`, `backdrop-proposal.yaml`) and
`tools/art/storm_layer_units.py pod canister` (`cargo-pod-final-r76-a`, `canister-final-r76-a`, `.png/.gif`).
Generated by code, not by an image model: the briefs below are what the generators were built to show (artist briefs
for a polished repaint). Every band, haze, cloud and glow is a smooth gradient (far more than 32 steps), no ordered
dither, drawn once (the 2026-10-30 dither audit); every glow additive.

- **backdrop** — Jupiter's storm layer under the cloud cities, no ground, in round 72's L38 grade (Q10 a):
  storm-dark blue-grey and slate bands far below, broad purple-grey cloud masses lit from the top left with dark
  creases between them (one purple-grey for every cloud of the level: the user's round-72 rule that clouds at
  different altitudes do not have different colours), mean luminance about 75, the darkest windows about 50. The deep
  layer is one continuous field down the level: the storm's top (the heads a step lighter) under Aurelia's wreck,
  which sinks far above as a dark burning shape (ART-C2's sinking city in the storm's cooler grade, its fires dimmed);
  the heavy storm floor; the floor under the storm cells; the Great Red Spot's rim far off on the right as a vast
  curving wall of cloud lit rust from inside the spot, a dark rust swirl beyond it and a rust glow spilling over the
  floor; the breakout, the cloud tops thinning over the last section until the gaps open on black sky with stars and
  Io's yellow disc (sulphur yellow, darker lava spots, a few orange vents, lit from the top left) rising into the top
  half. Deep pieces: two towering cloud walls and two storm cells seen from above (an overshooting dome ringed with
  lower billows, the anvil smeared out on the wind, the cell's shadow on the floor), each lit from inside by lightning:
  soft blue-white flashes where the cloud is thickest, a short bolt fragment on the strongest, three flashes in a 4 s
  loop. Low-air in the first section: Aurelia's deck plates, a cable coil and torn balloon fabric (ART-C2's fall
  debris) falling past in the storm's shade. High-air in the heavy sections: pale scud streaks drawn additively at
  the layer's 40 %. The lightning column's tell and strike are round 73's (`tools/art/act6_gear.py lightning`).
- **cargo-pod** — Aurelia's freight pod torn loose from the falling city: an octagonal-section container in tarnished
  gold with soot-darkened cream side panels, three gold bands, a torn cable stub at one end and two dead lamp lenses,
  36×36 at 16 turns of a slow tumble; dark against the storm (its lights dead); a lightning flash outlines it in
  blue-white for half a second (an additive outline per tumble frame); broken open in air: gold shell chips flung out,
  a hot orange core, a grey puff rising and thinning.
- **canister** — The CDF supply canister dropped through the storm: a CDF-grey capsule with the blue band, orange end
  stripes, a white panel and short fins under a round parachute of white and CDF-blue gores, six risers, the canopy
  trailing up the screen; 64×128 centred on the canister, the rig swaying ±9° in a 2 s loop. The latch (the 10 HP
  target) is a separate overlay: an orange clamp across the canister's top with an amber lamp (round 76 QD-4 b, the
  canister opening by itself, simply leaves it out). Released: a spark at the latch, the canister's halves swinging
  open and falling away, the canopy torn loose drifting up the screen as it shrinks and fades, a grey puff.
