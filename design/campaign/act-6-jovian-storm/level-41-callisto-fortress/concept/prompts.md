### backdrop-final-r79-a

- **Brief:** Callisto's surface from straight above in vacuum, the Sun low in the top left (about 21° up): every
  structure throws a shadow about 2.6 times its height to the bottom right. **No Jupiter** (the user, 2026-11-01): the
  ice covers the screen from the first frame. Dark blue-grey ice saturated with craters of every size, their sunward
  inner walls in shadow and their far rims lit, a fifth of them young with bright fresh-ice rims and rays. A crater-rim
  outpost: a small deck with three SAM pads, a black blockhouse with a red lamp and a thin mast. The outer causeway's
  gatehouse: a black block over the road, its roof overgrown by dark teal Vrell tissue with glowing veins round four
  grafted turrets. The road forks into two raised causeways (slate decks, gold edge lamps) across a crevasse field:
  black-blue branching cracks with pale frost lips. The outer wall: a long black wall with gold coping and crenels, two
  portals for the causeways, a central octagonal tower (the Sentinel's), its left half buried under a spreading mass of
  Vrell growth with teal veins. Inside, the citadel is black and gold on decks a step lighter than its units (slate
  blue-grey plates with joints, hazard stripes on the edges): the outer bailey with its lanes converging on Gate One.
  **Three gates**, each a black wall across the screen with gold coping and lamps, octagonal gate towers with slate top
  pads (the pylons stand on them), black bastions with dark roofs, a 150 px gateway between two posts with gold and red
  lamps, and before it an apron with an archive core's well (a recessed round well, a gold ring, dark teal glass, six
  teal lamps). The hangar decks: a launch deck up the middle (gold rails, chevrons, white edge lights), long ribbed black
  hangar roofs either side with open bays toward the deck, a dimmed Talon parked in each bay on a lit floor. The citadel
  ring: the ring road (gold lane dashes, edge lamps) curving round the citadel core's black wall, its roof beyond with
  plating seams, a gold strip, vent blocks, dishes and red lamps; barracks roofs on the outer side; a SAM terrace. The
  inner court: a paved court with lamp posts, a dark walkway with a gold medallion to the Halo's mount, a low colonnade
  along the left, a Graft nest's growth in a corner, and Varga's old research wing on the right, its roof cracked and
  fallen in round the lab vault's lit floor. The Halo's mount: a round plinth with a gold rim and lamps, a recessed ring
  channel with twelve radial ribs and a gold circle where the ring turns, the main core's dark glass at its centre.
  Helix HQ: stepped black roofs of three heights with raised blocks, gold coping, dishes and tall lattice comm masts

- **The doors:** each gateway's blast doors, two black leaves with heavy gold banding and the Ascendancy crest split

- **Readability:** the units are gloss black with gold (the elite pylons and the Halo) or gunmetal with gold (the
