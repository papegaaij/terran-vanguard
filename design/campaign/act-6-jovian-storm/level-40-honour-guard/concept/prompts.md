### backdrop-final-r78-a

- **Brief:** the Callisto approach from straight above, in vacuum: black space with sparse, mostly faint stars and two
  moons as points. At the start Jupiter's huge disc fills the bottom right, its limb running from the bottom left
  corner up to the right edge: a cream-orange crescent of bands along the limb (cream, ochre, rust), a thin bright
  haze line on it, the rest its brown-black night side with faint bands; the CDF carrier group far off over the night
  side as tiny dark hulls nose up with white strobes and blue engines (no red lights). Jupiter sinks out over the
  right edge; an Ascendancy picket station far off on the right: a gloss-black ring on three spokes round a gold hub,
  amber lamps, a white beacon. Then Callisto rises at the top: a huge dark blue-grey ice disc with a sharp limb (no
  atmosphere), its near side sunlit by a low sun behind the ship, saturated with small craters, bright specks of fresh
  ice, a pale multi-ring basin; its lit lower limb crosses the screen behind the duel. Three gold glints far ahead (the
  aces) flash in turn, then climb away out of the top. Callisto's orbital ring crosses below: a dark lattice band
  bowing like the limb, gold rails, two black hexagonal relay platforms with gold rims and dishes, gold and white
  lamps. Last the ice line: the terminator comes down the disc, sunlit ice below it, the night side above with the
  black citadel cut into the ice, only its lights showing: gold window strips, a ring of lamps round the keep, an
  outer line of bunker lamps on an arc, red beacons blinking on the towers.
- **Readability:** the gloss-black aces with heavy gold over Callisto's sunlit ice (the brightest element behind the
  duel, mean about 40 on the screen, 78 in its brightest window), the units' gold and amber bullets over Jupiter's
  cream (the crescent kept a band, the night side dark); no red in the backdrop before the citadel (the sniper's line
  is red).
