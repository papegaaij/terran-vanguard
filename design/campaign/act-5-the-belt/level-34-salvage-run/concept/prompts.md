  *Sable Wren* wedged against a hulk; one huge Helix freighter filling the screen, three engine bells, its hold open to
  space (the doors peeled back, a cradle with four clamp seats), the fore deck with its road and the bridge block; the
  tangle, frigates and hulks of both fleets crossing, the prospector's tender *Old Marta* (rusty ochre) half under a
  Helix hull; then clear space, the last plates falling behind, Ceres ahead as a hard white point and beside it the Iron
  Sovereign's ring of gold lights. A few emergency lights blink red. The roads jog sideways at the hull plates' seams.
  Posterized per image.
