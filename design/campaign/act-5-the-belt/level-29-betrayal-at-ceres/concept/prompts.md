# Level 29 – Betrayal at Ceres: prompts and briefs

## Round 62 — production (M9 part B)

Generators: `tools/art/backdrop_l29.py` (`backdrop-final-r62-a.png/.gif`, `backdrop-proposal.yaml`) and
`tools/art/belt_units.py clamp launch` (`clamp-gun-final-r62-a.png/.gif`). Generated by code, not by an image model: the brief below is what the generator was built to show (an artist brief for a polished repaint).

- **backdrop** — Ceres Hub's dock canyon seen from straight above in harsh sunlight from the top left, a step lighter
  than Act 4: a canyon cut into the dwarf planet's crust, warm brown-grey cratered regolith on both rims with patches of
  grey Coalition hull plating and tanks, the left wall in shadow and its shadow across the canyon floor, the right wall
  sunlit; the floor is grey deck plating with a pale guide strip, amber guide lights, pipe runs and cargo stacks along
  the walls; small cool-white rim lamps. Coalition blocks are grey-white modules with window strips and white lamps; the
  Helix dock block and pad are charcoal with gold trim and red lamps; Block C bridges the canyon with three turret pads
  on its roof and its lamps switching white to red; the red blocks beyond are lit red. Dock 9: a berth cradle with four
  clamp seats round Helix's prison tender (a long charcoal hull with gold bands, a blunt bow, two drive bells, its hold's
  two doors sliding open onto a lit hold). The canyon ends in a ragged sunlit crust edge; beyond, girder docks and the
  hub's outer ring over open space: black with the belt's sunlit dust band, stars, Ceres's cratered limb and the CDF
  transport. Low-air traffic: an industrial yellow-grey ore hauler with ochre ore boxes, CDF Stormhawk pairs, Talon pairs.
- **clamp-gun** — a gunmetal turret dome on a docking clamp, a gold collar, one barrel, a red lamp (40×40, 16 headings);
  the clamp a charcoal arm with gold hinge plates and a two-fingered jaw gripping the tender's flank (96×56, left and
  right), springing open with a white jet of gas and sparks when the gun dies.
- **rook-launch** — Rook's Ember (the CDF ship C airframe in Rook's navy and orange scheme) rising out of the tender's
  open hold: growing from small and shadowed to full size in the sun.

`--bench <s> --settings <scratch>/settings.properties --level 29 --invulnerable --loadout
front=ion-beam:4,rear=tail-gun:3,left=micro-missile-pod:3,right=micro-missile-pod:3,generator=3,shield=2,armor=3
--special emp-burst:2` (the balance plan's Level 29 fit; auto-fire on, the loaner Bulwark in the escort slot, the ship

recording − 5 s up to the halt (the clock stands in it). PNGs: frames of a `--shots <tick>,…` run of the same start

level time (60 ticks a second; past the halt the tick runs ahead of the stopped clock).
