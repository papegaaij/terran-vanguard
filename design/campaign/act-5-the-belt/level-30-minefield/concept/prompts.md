# Level 30 – Minefield: concept prompts and briefs

## Round 63 — production: the shipping-lane backdrop and the belt's large rocks (M9 part C)

Generators: `tools/art/backdrop_l30.py` (`backdrop-final-r63-a.png/.gif`, `backdrop-proposal.yaml`) and
`tools/art/belt_units_c.py rocks` (`belt-rocks-final-r63-a.png/.gif`). Generated by code, not by an image model: the
briefs below are what the generators were built to show (artist briefs for a polished repaint).

- **backdrop** — The shipping lane out of Ceres, space only (no ground), in the belt's lighter grade (round 60 A.18 a):
  near-black space with a warm sunlit dust band (umber-tan) winding down the screen left of centre, sparse stars,
  far rock specks. Deep layer pieces: Ceres's cratered sunlit globe sliding away at the bottom right at the start, a few
  distant hazed rocks, three tiny convoy hulls far ahead marked only by gold bow and red running lights, the open belt's
  scatter of lit rocks in a brighter haze at the end. Low-air: beacon buoys down both edges (a white-grey float drum
  with orange hazard bands, a short mast with two solar vanes and a white strobe flashing once in 2 s), some shot out
  (mast snapped, scorched, dark), the last beacon (a tall mast with a green strobe), big rocks tumbling slowly past,
  darkened and cooled so they read as background. High-air: sparse dark grit lit on its top-left edge, pale dust motes.
  Harsh sun from the top left; posterized, wide gradients ordered-dithered.
- **belt-rocks** — The belt's large rocks: lumpy warm grey-brown regolith with craters and flat facets, lit hard from
  the top left, `belt-large-a` about 88×80 and `-b` about 66×60, 16 frames of a slow spin each (part B's rock model at
  a larger radius). The dead beacon buoy: the lane buoy's float drum and bent mast, scorched and dark, tumbling (32×32,
  16 frames); shot open: its panels flung out in a ring with grey dust (vacuum: no smoke) and the hidden crate's amber
  light at the core.
