# Level 35 – Iron Sovereign: prompts and briefs

## Round 68 — production: the Level 35 backdrop (M9 part H)

Generator: `tools/art/backdrop_l35.py` (`backdrop-final-r68-a.png/.gif`; its places for step H2 in
`backdrop-proposal.yaml`). Generated by code, not by an image model: the brief below is what the generator was built to
show (an artist brief for a polished repaint).

- **backdrop-final** — Ceres orbit seen straight down in the belt's lighter grade, the sun at the top left. Open space
  with the sunlit dust band; far below, Ceres's sunlit curve with Ceres Hub's lights red on its night side; loyalist
  fighter wrecks and a dead Coalition-grey patrol boat drifting, dimmed; in the escort screen the Sovereign's charcoal
  and gold tenders and a docking spar far below. Then the battle station's outer hull, a long charcoal keel the ship
  crosses (Q.11 b: gunmetal and charcoal with gold): panelled plating of slightly different heights, two armour belts
  along its sides with gold inner trim and red running lights, gold-trimmed transverse frames, raised armour slabs with
  gold edges, conduit runs, vent grilles, hatches with gold rings, heat-sink fins, gold sensor domes; hexagonal SAM
  sockets and octagonal Rail Bunker sockets where the hull guns stand; docking clamps holding a white-and-orange
  lifeboat; a loyalist fighter crashed on the plating with a CDF beacon on its back. The hull begins at a gold-lipped
  bulkhead with spar stubs over space and ends at another, its shoulders narrowing into a neck that runs north to the
  station's centre under the ring assembly (ART-H1's rings and core), Ceres's north pole far below the halted station.
  After the kill, broken hull plates drift on open space.

## Round 68 — production: the Ceres lifeboat (M9 part H)

Generator: `tools/art/l35_props.py lifeboat` (`lifeboat-final-r68-a.png`). Rendered from a model (the frozen `render`
package), lit from the top left.
