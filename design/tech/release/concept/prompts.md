# Release – concept prompts

The application icon is generated by [tools/art/app_icon.py](../../../../tools/art/app_icon.py)
from the Stormhawk's SDF model (`tools/concept/vfx_r08.py`, `render/models.py`); rerun it to
regenerate the icon files and the review sheet. Do not hand-edit the generated files.

## app-icon-r35-a

The application icon (window, Windows exe, macOS app), shown at 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256 and
512 px on a dark and a light background.

Prompt: `app icon, rounded square tile, top-down orthographic render of a sleek white-grey sci-fi
jet interceptor pointing up, swept wings with blue tips, long needle fuselage with a blue spine
stripe and an orange nose band, dark blue glass canopy, twin dark engine nacelles, on a deep
navy gradient tile with a soft blue glow behind the ship, rounded steel-blue bevelled border
with a thin light inner line, key light from the top left, 1990s pre-rendered CGI look, glossy
specular highlights, crisp silhouette readable at 16 px`

Negative prompt: `perspective view, three-quarter view, text, letters, watermark, photo,
painterly, cartoon outline, flat vector, busy background`

Artist notes: the ship fills about 80 % of the tile; at 16 and 32 px only its silhouette and the
blue wing tips have to read. The trim takes the title logo's steel blue (#3361A8) and light
steel (#B7C7DA).
