# Dr. Elena Varga

## Summary

Aegis Wing's intel officer and the Coalition's leading xenobiologist. She explains the enemy:
weak points, behaviours, what is coming in the next level. The first to suspect the
Ascendancy.

## Design

- **Role**: voice of the hangar intel panel (see [ui](../../../ui/README.md)); radio lines on
  new enemy types and bosses ("hit the glowing sacs"); occasional mission briefings.
- **Personality**: brilliant, curious, a little too fascinated by the Vrell. Talks fast when
  excited. Speaks truth to authority, then gets overruled.
- **Voice**: precise, scientific, sometimes forgets there is a war on ("Beautiful. Also
  deadly. Mostly deadly.").
- **Background**: 40s, Hungarian, worked on Helix-funded gate research until 2179 — she left
  after the "missing" survey mission and never believed the accident story.
- **Arc**: notices in Act 3 that the Vrell avoid Helix sites; Okafor tells her to keep quiet.
  In Act 4 she decodes the Helix frequencies on the beacons. In Act 5 she says what she sees, on every
  channel (Okafor's Act 4 promise), and sees the bargain on Vorne's face (*Act 5* below). In Act 6 she watches the Vrell eat Aurelia and reads, in
  Vorne's own files, why they came (*Act 6* below). In Act 7 she
  is the one who understands the Choir's fear and hints at the Silence.
- **Gameplay tie-in**: each level's intel line from Varga names one practical tip (weak point,
  recommended weapon trait, dominant attack direction), consistent with the level's threat
  profile.

### Act 5 (draft)

*Draft, step `m9-d-story` (2026-10-27), **approved in round 60**. The texts are the
[act's](../../../campaign/act-5-the-belt/README.md) outro and the level documents'.*

- **What she knew**: at Helix she saw Vorne at the gate-research reviews he funded; he was sixty when
  she left in 2179.
- **L31**: the refugees' story of Helix's cures (Vrell biotech that healed the belt's miners and
  bought their stations) is the first time she hears the bargain described from the inside; as the
  xenobiologist she knows what such a cure is grown from.
- **L33**: her hangar intel warns to bring spare power against the leeches (the
  [generator](../../../player/generator/README.md#enemy-drain-effects)'s drain).
- **The act outro, her Act 5 beat**: after the recruitment she has seen Vorne's face on the feed:
  he looks forty, and he was sixty when she left Helix. The cures are on him. It is the one place the
  act ties the bargain to the man.

### Act 6 (draft)

*Draft, step `m10-d-story` (2026-10-31), **decided 2026-10-31 (round 72)** (Q3 a, Q12 a). The texts are the
level documents', the L42 briefing's and the [act's](../../../campaign/act-6-jovian-storm/README.md) outro.*

- **L36, the hybrids**: she names them (the [Chimera](../../../enemies/air/chimera/README.md): "*I call them
  Chimeras*"). The Ascendancy grew Vrell tissue into its own airframes; she explains the healing, and as the
  xenobiologist she knows what it costs the men inside: said once, never shown (the tone guide: no gore).
- **L37, the turn** (Q3 a): she is shaken, and she says the story's line: the Vrell "*were never his partners. Only a
  door*". Before it, inside Aurelia's traffic net, she **keeps the city's identification codes**: the fleet builds the
  [IFF spoofer](../../../player/systems/README.md#act-6-the-iff-spoofer-and-the-core-tiers-draft) from them (the L39
  visit). Over the fall she is the one who notes that Aurelia Traffic is off the air.
- **L38–L39**: intel as usual (lightning, the Harrows, Io's vents and the Striders).
- **L40, the approach**: over Callisto's ice she says she had an office in Helix HQ once, three floors down (the
  level's line): the place she left in 2179 is the one she breaks into next.
- **L41, the files** (Q12 a): the act's reveal is hers. Her uplink spike on Helix HQ's data core (the level's `FILES`
  secondary, flown over and held) pulls Vorne's files, and her lines come as they arrive: the deal seen from the Vrell
  side. They offered the biotech for passage to the inner system; they were not invading, they were running; the
  files call what they run from **the Silence**. It is named here, once, sharply, never described. She was at Helix
  when the 2179 survey "went missing"; the files are the first proof she has that it did not.
- **L42's briefing, page 2**: from the files: what the deal was, what Vorne thinks he still holds, the 2179 survey
  that made the contact. She refers to the Silence; she does not explain it (no one can).
- **The act outro**: the Silence is her beat: the gate is not only the Vrell's door; they came through it running.
  It points to Act 7, where she understands the Choir's fear.

### Portrait brief

White woman in her forties, dark auburn hair pinned up with a stylus through it, thin wire
glasses, intense eyes. Lab coat over a CDF intelligence uniform, a holographic display casting
green light on her face. Neutral: focused; alternates: excited (half-smile, leaning to the
screen), alarmed (hand to mouth). Late-90s pre-rendered CGI style.
