# The Vrell

## Summary

A biomechanical hive species. Vrell ships are not built but grown: chitin shells, living
engines, glowing sacs of plasma. They think as one through a collective voice humans call "the
Choir". They come through the Tether Gate in endless numbers — and, as the player learns at the
very end, they are running from something.

## Design

### Nature

- **Hive mind.** Individual Vrell organisms have no personality. The Choir is the sum of
  them, heard over human comms as layered, singing whispers.
- **Grown technology.** Ships, weapons and structures are bred organisms. They heal, adapt,
  spawn smaller organisms, and die rather than break: destroyed Vrell dissolve, burst or wither.
- **Not evil, alien.** They do not taunt or bargain. Humans are an obstacle and a resource.
- **Adaptive.** Across the campaign they adapt to human tactics: armour that regrows,
  organisms that mimic human ships, underwater and burrowing variants per setting.

### Goals (known and hidden)

- Known (Acts 1–4): conquest and harvesting of the solar system.
- Revealed (Act 6, from L37): the Ascendancy was only a door; they were never allies.
- Hinted (first named at L41 in Vorne's files, then through Act 7): they are fleeing "the Silence" — something that emptied their home worlds.
  The Choir's lines grow frightened near the end. Never shown, never explained.

### Visual language

- **Shapes**: organic curves, segmented shells, mandibles, fins, tendrils; asymmetric details
  on symmetric bodies. Nothing has a straight edge.
- **Surface**: dark, glossy chitin (deep brown, bruised purple, oil-slick sheen) over soft
  tissue.
- **Glow**: bioluminescent teal and violet — weak points, engines and weapons glow. Weak points
  are always the brightest glow (gameplay readability).
- **Movement**: pulsing, undulating, sudden darts. Engines are glowing sacs that pulse; no
  flame.
- **Death**: burst into glowing fluid and chitin fragments; large organisms collapse and dissolve
  over a second or two.
- **Structures**: hives, spires and "roots" that grow into human cities and moons; organic
  towers with glowing veins.

### Doctrine (as seen in-game)

| Trait | In-game behaviour |
|---|---|
| Swarm | Many weak units in flocking formations; waves arrive in quick succession. |
| Overwhelm | Attacks from multiple directions at once, including from behind as the war goes on. |
| Spawn | Carriers and hives release smaller units until destroyed. |
| Regenerate | Some units heal unless damage is sustained; weak points must be hit. |
| Adapt | Setting-specific variants (burrowers on Mars, swimmers on Europa). |
| Sacrifice | Kamikaze units, ramming, bursting into damaging clouds on death. |

### Act 6: the turn (draft)

*Draft, step `m10-d-story` (2026-10-31), **decided 2026-10-31 (round 72)** (Q3 a, Q12 a). The rule in play is
the [enemies'](../../../enemies/README.md#the-three-way-war); the lines are the level and boss documents'.*

- **Before the turn** (L36, L37's first 100 s): Vrell and Ascendancy fly side by side, as in the belt; the growth
  already creeps over Aurelia's decks.
- **The turn** (L37, Aurelia): the Choir says "*…the one who opened… is no longer needed… the door is ours…*" and the
  Vrell turn on the Ascendancy. Not a betrayal in their terms: they were never partners; the Ascendancy was only a door. From
  then on chosen Vrell waves hunt Ascendancy craft (latching, ramming, firing at them, Q3 a) and the growth eats the
  gold city until it falls. They still fight the CDF too: the war is three-way, not an alliance with anybody.
- **What they spare**: the hybrids. The tissue grown into the Chimeras, the Harrows and the Graft Turrets is theirs,
  so they never hunt them; at L42 the Vrell half of Vorne's flagship turns the ship against him, and the lifeboat it
  grows carries him toward the gate (the seed of [Vorne's Chimera](../../../enemies/bosses/README.md), Act 7).
- **Why they came** (L41, Q12 a): Vorne's files hold the deal from their side. They offered the biotech for passage
  through the gate to the inner system; they were not invading, they were running, from something the files call
  **the Silence**. Named once, by Varga; never shown, never explained. The Choir itself says nothing of it until Act 7.
- **Doctrine in Act 6**: swarm and overwhelm as ever (Motes, Skitters, Lampreys, Wraiths), *sacrifice* in the
  rammers that now dive at the Ascendancy as readily as at the ship, *adapt* in the storm-riding swarms of
  Jupiter's bands ([Jovian system](../../../world/jovian/README.md)).
