# The Jovian Ascendancy

## Summary

A secessionist movement of the outer colonies, centred on the Jovian cloud cities and funded by
the Helix Dynamics corporation. Led by Chairman Silas Vorne. It secretly re-opened the Tether
Gate, offering the Vrell a way in in exchange for Vrell biotech that would make the Ascendancy
the new masters of humanity. The hidden enemy of the first half of the game (twist variant A,
see [twist](../../twist/README.md)).

## Design

### Goals and culture

- Officially: independence for the outer colonies, "freedom from Earth".
- Really: Vorne wants to rule what remains of humanity after the Vrell have broken the
  Coalition, with Vrell biotech (regenerating hulls, living weapons, longevity) as his edge.
- Culture: elite, wealthy, corporate-aristocratic. Believes itself the future of humanity.
  Uniforms, crests, grand architecture, propaganda broadcasts.

### Visual language

- **Shapes**: angular, faceted, knife-edged, stealth-like. Long thin hulls, forward-swept wings,
  sharp chines. Clearly human-built, but more elegant and more expensive than Coalition gear.
- **Colours**: gloss black, gold trim and gold crest (a rising sun over a ringed planet), red
  running lights.
- **Engines**: hot orange-red thrust with a thin, focused flame.
- **Late-war hybrids** (Act 6): Ascendancy hulls grafted with Vrell tissue — black and gold
  plating with teal glowing growths. The visual proof of the bargain.
- **Architecture**: cloud cities with gilded domes, tall spires, platforms suspended under
  balloons; Callisto HQ as a fortified black citadel.

### Doctrine (as seen in-game)

| Trait | In-game behaviour |
|---|---|
| Precision | Fewer, tougher units; aimed shots instead of bullet spam. |
| Ambush | Cloaked units, attacks from the sides and rear, units that wait until the player passes. |
| Tech advantage | Shields on enemies, drones, rail guns that telegraph and then cross the screen. |
| Command | Squad leaders: destroying the leader breaks the formation. |

### Story role

- Acts 1–2: invisible. Mentioned only as a political footnote in briefings.
- Acts 3–4: hints — Vrell avoid Helix sites on Mars, human relay beacons under Europa.
- End of Act 4 / Act 5: revealed when Ascendancy ships attack Aegis Wing; Vorne's broadcast opens
  Act 5 and the faction fights in the open in the belt (*Act 5* below).
- Act 6: the hunt for Vorne; the Vrell turn on the Ascendancy.
- Act 7: the faction is broken; only Vorne himself remains, fused with his Vrell lifeboat as
  Vorne's Chimera (L48).

### Act 5: the Ascendancy in the belt (draft)

*Draft, step `m9-d-story` (2026-10-27), **pending the user's review in round 60**. The units' numbers and
rules are in their own documents; this section only shows how the faction's doctrine reads in the act
([Act 5](../../../campaign/act-5-the-belt/README.md), the [belt](../../../world/belt/README.md)).*

- **Why the belt**: holding the belt's mining stations cuts Earth off from the outer system. Helix
  Dynamics has its belt yards at Ceres, and the **Iron Sovereign** battle station holds Ceres orbit
  ([boss](../../../enemies/bosses/iron-sovereign/README.md), L35).
- **How it holds the stations**: Helix bought loyalty with Vrell biotech cures for the miners (the L31
  refugees' story), and Vorne's broadcast asks every station to choose. Ceres Hub declares for the
  Ascendancy and half its garrison mutinies (L29); the mutineers fly Talons, and some Ceres pilots in
  repainted CDF craft waver and peel off (round 60 Q10 b).
- **Look**: the Ascendancy in the open, gloss black and gold with a strong rim light and red running
  lights so it reads over dark rock (Q11 a; the rule is the [enemies](../../../enemies/README.md#factions-and-visual-language)
  document's). The unmarked grey of Acts 3–4 is gone; the Warden Tank of L19 returns in livery in L34
  (J1, [Warden Tank](../../../enemies/ground/warden-tank/README.md)).
- **Doctrine in Act 5's units**:

| Trait | Act 5 units |
|---|---|
| Precision | [Talon](../../../enemies/air/talon/README.md) (paired aimed shots), [Rail Bunker](../../../enemies/ground/rail-bunker/README.md) (a rail shot along a telegraphed line), [Hornet](../../../enemies/air/hornet/README.md) and [SAM Nest](../../../enemies/ground/sam-nest/README.md) (shootable homing diamonds) |
| Ambush | the turrets of Ceres Hub that wake when the hub turns (L29), [Minelayers](../../../enemies/space/minelayer/README.md) and [Rail Serpents](../../../enemies/air/rail-serpent/README.md) from behind (L30), [Sentinel Towers](../../../enemies/ground/sentinel-tower/README.md) in side galleries (L32) |
| Tech advantage | light hex [shields](../../../enemies/README.md#enemy-shields) on crewed craft (Talon, Hornet, [Gilded Gunship](../../../enemies/air/gilded-gunship/README.md), the Sovereign's core; Q1 a); drones: [Dart Drone](../../../enemies/air/dart-drone/README.md) streams, [Shard Drone](../../../enemies/space/shard-drone/README.md) links, [Buzzsaw Drone](../../../enemies/air/buzzsaw-drone/README.md); [Strider](../../../enemies/ground/strider/README.md) walkers |
| Command | the Talon's gold-striped squad leader: kill it and the wing scatters |

- **Fewer, tougher, plus drones**: the crewed craft stay few; the expendable Dart Drones the Gunships,
  stations and the Sovereign launch carry the count with the Vrell's popcorn (Q6 b).
- **The Vrell beside them**: Asteroid Mites, Mote Swarms and Skitters fight in the same waves, but the
  alliance is uneasy: in L33 the [Void Leeches](../../../enemies/space/void-leech/README.md) latch onto Talons
  too, and the Choir speaks of Vorne with indifference ([the Choir](../../characters/the-choir/README.md)).
- **Captured tech**: the Generator Mk V "Helix" is Helix tech salvaged in L34 and sold from the L35 visit
  (Q12 c, the [generator](../../../player/generator/README.md)).
